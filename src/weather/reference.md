# Weather: reference

Moved out of `AGENTS.md` (#723) so the auto-loaded doc keeps only invariants, gotchas and ownership. Not auto-loaded: read it before changing this area. Where it disagrees with the code or a spec, they win.

- **Cache** (`weather_forecast`, `service.ts`): one row per rounded location and zone (`time_zone`, 0057: a home and a destination at one cell in two zones are two rows), no user id: the last good forecast (`jsonb`), `fetched_at`, `attempted_at`. Fresh for an hour; **past that the cached answer is served at once and refreshed in the background**; **only a cold miss** (no answer kept) waits, for at most the fetcher's 10 s. **A one-shot decision never acts on a stale forecast**: "Plan my week" (route and MCP) reads with `{ fresh: true }` (`forecastFor`/`userWeather`'s `read`), waiting for a stale row's refresh (falling back to it only if that fails). **Batch jobs refresh together first**: the daily re-plan and the morning reminders call `refreshForecastsFor` with the minute's people, which reads every person's settings and row in one statement (`findWeathersWithForecast`, `weatherWithForecastSql` as a list), refreshes their distinct places `BATCH_REFRESH_CONCURRENCY` (4) at a time within one `BATCH_REFRESH_DEADLINE_MS` (12 s), and answers each person's weather (no one's weather is read again; one whose place failed or overran is read without `fresh`): N places cost about one fetch (a skipped reminder minute is still sent within `LATE_LIMIT_MINUTES`). Pages keep serve-stale. A new scheduled reader of the forecast joins its batch's refresh (`refresh-batch.spec.ts`, `push-reminders.spec.ts`). A failed refresh keeps the last good answer and waits 10 minutes before asking again. **A kept answer to another question is no answer** (`mismatch`): a forecast whose `timeZone` is not its key's or normals whose `years` are not `normalYears` of today is never served, however young, nor as the fallback: the ask waits like a cold miss (`service.spec.ts`). One refresh per location at a time per process, and the row is not read while one runs: an ask meanwhile is served what the refresh started from (a cold one joins it), so an ask that read the stale row just before the save can never fetch again. That discipline is `createLocationCache` (`location-cache.ts`, `location-cache.spec.ts`), used by both caches: a new Open-Meteo answer kept per location goes through it, not a copy. A background refresh never rejects (failures are logged), and **the app's close awaits `WeatherService.settled()`** before ending the pool, so an in-flight refresh still saves. No timer: only asks start refreshes. Specs counting fetches after a stale ask await `t.weather.settled()`, never a poll (`refreshed` in `weather.spec.ts`, `climate-normals.spec.ts`); one asserting what concurrent asks got during a refresh holds the stand-in's answer (`stub.hold(true)`) until they are in. **One read for the person**: `userWeather` reads the settings and the active location's cache row in one statement (`findWeatherWithForecast`: activeLocation's rule in SQL, used only when it agrees with the JS rule) and hands the row in as `known`; the cache decides from it unless a read or refresh of that place is under way or this process wrote the row after it was read (`written`), so the single-flight rule holds (`location-cache.spec.ts`). The summary is 2 statements. The read is a scalar subquery (`weatherWithForecastSql`, via `readWeatherWithForecast` and `userWeatherFrom`), so `ideasFor` carries it in its one statement (JSON timestamps come back as strings; the reader makes Dates). **A table, not memory**: restarts start warm, overlapping deploys share it, and the last good answer survives a restart. **Pruned nightly** (`src/maintenance/weather-prune.ts`, `weather_prune`, #709): a forecast or normals row whose coordinates no `user_weather` home or here and no trip destination has, and whose `attempted_at` is over 30 days old (`WEATHER_CACHE_RETENTION_DAYS`, the process's clock like the stamp), is deleted; matched on coordinates alone (a pre-#678 trip has no zone), a row in use is kept at any age (`test/integration/weather-prune.spec.ts`).

- **Matching** (`src/weather/match.ts`, pure, `match.spec.ts` across seasons and occasions): `weatherNeeds(day, occasion, offset)` reads the occasion's window, its feels-like range plus the personal offset, and sets targets: the **torso** (top or one-piece plus layer, combined 1 to 9: one step per 4 °C down from 26 °C; a tee 2, a sweater under a parka 9) and **legs and feet** (one garment, 1 to 4: at most 3, ordinary trousers and closed shoes passing, until a cold snap below `COLD_SNAP`, -10 °C; legs may run 2 warmer, feet 1, judged against the uncapped target (`limbsCeiling`, up to 5) so the cap never makes insulated boots too warm in winter; a workout's are judged only too warm above `COLD_SNAP`, `ActiveDemand`, #647; below it `legsTooColdForRun` keeps running shorts out of the generator's draws); **a layer** when the window's coolest and warmest hours are two torso steps apart (judged with the layer at the coolest hours, without at the warmest; a swing inside the heat asks for nothing); **rain** when an hour of the window reaches 50 %, wanting a water-resistant layer, footwear or accessory (not a bag), **unless the umbrella answers it** (`umbrella`: every rain hour above `SHELL_CEILING`, 22 °C feels-like, or, through `withRainGear`, a water-resistant hat or cap in the pool (`rainGear`; a watch or belt does not count), which every surface applies from `rainGearFor` (`src/web/weather/queries.ts`: ideas, Today, the weather resource, the calendar, the MCP tools, the week planner and its re-plan; a read made only when a day asks for water resistance; a trip's days read the gear on its packing list instead, whatever its state at home): then no outfit is scored on it and the idea lists say "Take the umbrella" once (#648). `assessOutfit(needs, garments)` lists the problems and a score (0 fits) and judges only the parts an outfit has; **a layer is judged on its own too**, not only in the sum: at most one step over the coolest hours' torso target less a tee (else too warm: no parka at 15 °C) and at most `LAYER_SPREAD` (3) over the tops under it (else too cold: no parka over a linen shirt). Every part is non-negative and additive, or the generator's `scoreFloor` breaks. **`real-days.spec.ts` pins what the rules add up to**: five real New York days against the demo closet (varied January trousers, an office outfit, no parka in October, no boots in July, three tops and no shell on a rainy July day); a recalibration starts with a row there (`docs/decisions.md` 2026-11-13); `matchGarment` counts an unknown warmth as the type's preset (its weight step included), else the role's usual. Availability (`isAvailable`) is separate: filter, then match.

- **Climate normals** (`src/weather/normals.ts`, pure, `normals.spec.ts`; `drizzle/0025_weather_normals.sql`): what a calendar day is usually like at a place, for **a trip's days past the forecast only**. From Open-Meteo's **historical archive** (`archive-api.open-meteo.com/v1/archive`, ERA5), not its biased climate API: one request for `NORMAL_YEARS` (10) whole years before the household's current one (`normalYears`) of daily highs, lows, feels-like and precipitation, at the rounded location, in the place's zone (~140 KB under the 512 KB cap). `climateNormals` averages each calendar day over `NORMAL_WINDOW_DAYS` (7) either side in every year (wrapping New Year; 29 February kept); the rain chance is the share of wet days (`WET_DAY_MM`, 1 mm). Cached in **`weather_normals`**, a table beside `weather_forecast` (another endpoint, answer and lifetime), **one row per location holding all 366 days**: the archive answers a date range, so any month costs the ten years' fetch anyway. Fresh for 30 days (`NORMALS_FRESH_FOR_MS`), with the same last-good, 10-minute retry and single-flight (`normalsFor`). **The typical day** (`typicalDay`) turns a day's normals into a `DayForecast` so `weatherNeeds`/`assessOutfit` judge it with the same inputs: 24 hours whose feels-like follow a clear day's curve (`diurnalPhase`, shared with the seed) from the low at 6:00 to the high at 15:00, every hour's precipitation chance the day's rain chance, the place's hours, a condition of only dry, rain or snow. **Never for Today, the calendar, the gallery's own days, the weekly plan or its re-plan**: those stay forecast-only (a typical day must never swap a planned outfit); only `tripForecast` and `ideasFor` with a trip's `place` ask for normals (`climate-normals.spec.ts` checks Today, a far gallery day and "Plan my week" fetch no archive; `week-plan.spec.ts`'s fake service rejects `normalsFor`). Logs: `Climate normals for 30.27,-97.74 America/Chicago: 366 days from 2016-2025 in 140 ms`, a failure like the forecast's.

## Layout

```
weather/   The weather, pure: location.ts (rounding to ~1 km, the only form a coordinate is kept or
           sent in), temperature.ts (units, the personal offset), forecast.ts (the forecast shape,
           WMO conditions, a clear day's curve), match.ts (what a day asks of an outfit for an
           occasion, and how an outfit answers it), normals.ts (climate normals and the typical day)
```
