# Weekly auto-plan

_Reference, not auto-loaded:_ [`reference.md`](reference.md) holds The planner; Writes; Undo; The daily re-plan; Seed.

`src/wardrobe/week.ts` and `src/wardrobe/week-planner.ts` (pure), `src/web/week-plan/`, `src/web/mcp/tools/week-plan.ts` (#16, plan section 12). "Plan my week" fills the week template's empty slots with the generator's outfits, and a daily re-plan swaps its own picks when the forecast changes or an outfit can no longer be worn. **The signed-in user's own, like the calendar**: no route takes `?ownerId=`, another's batch is a 404.

- **The week template is the one model of the week** (owner direction on #16: no second, disagreeing model). `week_template` (user, `weekday` 0 = Sunday to 6, `occasion`; `drizzle/0022_week_plan.sql`): per weekday at most one of `DAY_OCCASIONS` (the day's outfit: all day, work, daytime; the partial unique index `week_template_user_id_weekday_day_unique`) and any of the others around it (workout, evening, night out). #34a's `style_rhythm` ("work 3 a week", "evening 3 a month") is gone: counts cannot say which days to plan, and two stored models would disagree the first time one is edited. The rhythm is **derived** (`weeklyRhythm`: on how many weekdays each occasion comes round), shown read-only on the style page (linking to Profile › Your week) and answered by `get_style_profile` beside the template. The migration spread each weekly count onto weekdays (work from Monday, all day and daytime from the weekend back, the others spread Monday, Thursday, Saturday...) and dropped the monthly ones, which no weekday holds (only the seed's demo had rows; `week-plan-migration.spec.ts` (retired 2026-10-13)). Occasional evenings are planned by hand. One writer, `saveWeekTemplate` (replaced whole; the changes feed's `weekTemplate` through 0052's stamp, `weekTemplatesByUserIds`, #716); one form, `WeekTemplateBody`/`readWeekTemplateForm` (`template.ts`), which the seed posts through too. The editor is its own page, Profile › Your week (`GET /auth/profile/week`, `WeekTemplatePage`, `views.tsx`; a Profile section until #728, whose `#week` row now opens it and shows `weekTemplateSummary`): per weekday a select for the day's outfit and chips for the rest, **one Save** (a native `PostForm` to the same address, 303 to `/auth/profile/week?saved=1`), not save-on-change: seven rows are one decision, and a half-edited week must not be planned from.
- **Days away are not planned** (#684): `planMyWeek` drops the days a trip covers (`splitAwayDays`, `src/web/trips/away.ts`) before `emptySlots`, answers them as `away` (the API's and `plan_week`'s too), and the banner says "Not planned while you are away: ..." (`data-away-skipped`), never "Still empty"; a trip's create and any change of its dates remove the unworn auto entries on its days from today on in their own transaction (`createTripAt`, `updateTrip`; a past day's plan stays, still the person's to mark worn), and the re-plan removes any left there (a trip written by the seed or a fixture), all through `removeAutoEntries` (by ids or a day range; Undo's rule: the outfit it made goes once unheld), the re-plan's logged `..., N removed from days away`, never pushed; a person's or an agent's entry there stays.
- **Rain gear is the web layer's read** (#759): `weekRainGear` (`plan.ts`, over `rainGearFor`) reads the closet's gear once per plan or re-plan run, only when a slot being planned or judged asks for water resistance, and hands it to the pure planner as `WeekContext.rainGear`; the planner never scans its pool for it, so a hat answers a rainy day here as on Today.
- **`outfit_calendar.planned_by`** (`auto` | `agent` | `user` in rank order, default and every entry before #16 `user`; `agent` since #682, with `planned_by_token_id`): who owns the choice. Its values are written only through `src/web/calendar/planned-by.ts` (`chooserOf`, `takeOverSql`, `PERSONS_CHOICE`): re-choosing the same outfit takes an entry over only from a lower rank, a replace makes it the chooser's, the person's edits and wears make it `user`; only `auto` is ever re-planned. **Every writer that edits an entry makes it the person's**: `setEntryWorn` (marking or unmarking), `updateOutfit` (the calendar chip's edit link is the outfit form; every auto entry of the outfit), `setEntryOutfit` (Change, #69, which also removes the planner's outfit it replaced once unheld, through Undo's `removeUnheldOutfits`) and `insertEntry` when the person plans the outfit already auto on that day (the outfit page's Plan, the plan page's and the Saved tab's pick, `schedule_outfit`, a pick with that day: #77, see Calendar). **A person's pick or save of a planner-made outfit's garments makes the outfit theirs** (`adoptOutfit`, called by `createOutfit`'s reuse, which also raises the outfit's `origin`, #659; see Outfits): its entries stay the planner's, but Undo, a swap and Change no longer delete it (`outfit_created` is what `removeAutoEntries` passes to `removeUnheldOutfits`; `outfitIsHeld` is not widened: it answers "what references this outfit", a different question). Only `auto` entries are ever re-planned or undone. The row carries an Auto badge (`OccasionRow`, `data-auto`), `get_calendar` its `plannedBy`.
- **Planning's routes** (`routes.tsx`; API twins in `api-routes.ts`, #193b): the button, `plan_week` and `POST /week-plans` all plan through `planMyWeekNow`, never a path of their own. `POST /calendar/plan-week` with no template is a 303 to `/auth/profile/week`; else a 303 to `/calendar?week=<the Sunday of the batch's first planned day>&planned=<batch>#day-<first>` (#683: the page shows Sunday to Saturday, the plan covers today and six, so the first planned day may be next week), or `?planned=none`. Undo is a 303 to `/calendar?undone=N`. A banner row links its day's `#day-D` within the shown week, else `dayUrl`. `POST /auth/profile/week` refuses an occasion outside its field's set with a 400.
- **`/calendar` stays byte-stable**: the "Plan my week" button is static, and `?planned=` (the banner) and `?undone=` (a toast) are navigation state, stripped from the address (`StripFlags`).
- **Locks and order**: `planMyWeek` reads the forecast outside any lock, then everything else after `lockOwner`, so a double tap waits and finds its slots filled. Undo asks whose batch only when its delete found nothing (`NotFound('weekPlan')` from `undoWeekPlan` itself, #188d). `removeUnheldOutfits` is two statements, the lock then the delete: never fold them (a delete waiting on the lock judges `outfitIsHeld` by an older snapshot); it deletes only origin `idea`/`planner` outfits.
- **The re-plan** (`replan.ts`) is scheduled by `server.ts` only, never `createApp()` (the harness and CLIs never re-plan), and runs once a day per person, whoever asks: its claim commits in the same transaction as its work, so a failure leaves the day unclaimed (`ReplanRetries`). It swaps only an auto entry that can no longer be worn or an idea answering the weather better; the swap notice goes after the commit, one push per swap, never two.

## Gotchas

- **An auto entry is the planner's only until someone touches it.** `outfit_calendar.planned_by` is what keeps the daily re-plan and Undo off a person's choice (#16): a new writer that edits an entry (its outfit, its occasion, worn) must set it to `user` in the same transaction, as `setEntryWorn`, `updateOutfit` and `setEntryOutfit` do, or the next morning's re-plan may swap out what they chose. **Choosing what the planner chose is touching it**: `insertEntry` adopts an `auto` entry the person plans again (`ON CONFLICT ... DO UPDATE ... WHERE`), and `createOutfit` (every pick and save) adopts a planner-made outfit a person's save reuses (`adoptOutfit`, `adopt.ts`); a new path where the person picks or plans something that may already be the planner's goes through them, never around (an `ON CONFLICT DO NOTHING` of its own left #77's entries `auto`). Only the planner writes `auto` (through `pickIdea`'s `plannedBy`), and a new reader of "the planner's entries" filters `planned_by = 'auto'` rather than joining `week_plan_entry` alone (that row stays, as provenance, after the person takes the entry over).
