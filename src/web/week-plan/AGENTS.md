# Weekly auto-plan

`src/wardrobe/week.ts` and `src/wardrobe/week-planner.ts` (pure), `src/web/week-plan/`, `src/web/mcp/tools/week-plan.ts` (#16, plan section 12). "Plan my week" fills the week template's empty slots with the generator's outfits, and a daily re-plan swaps its own picks when the forecast changes or an outfit can no longer be worn. **The signed-in user's own, like the calendar**: no route takes `?ownerId=`, another's batch is a 404.

- **The week template is the one model of the week** (owner direction on #16: no second, disagreeing model). `week_template` (user, `weekday` 0 = Sunday to 6, `occasion`; `drizzle/0022_week_plan.sql`): per weekday at most one of `DAY_OCCASIONS` (the day's outfit: all day, work, daytime; the partial unique index `week_template_user_id_weekday_day_unique`) and any of the others around it (workout, evening, night out). #34a's `style_rhythm` ("work 3 a week", "evening 3 a month") is gone: counts cannot say which days to plan, and two stored models would disagree the first time one is edited. The rhythm is **derived** (`weeklyRhythm`: on how many weekdays each occasion comes round), shown read-only on the style page (linking to Profile › Your week) and answered by `get_style_profile` beside the template. The migration spread each weekly count onto weekdays (work from Monday, all day and daytime from the weekend back, the others spread Monday, Thursday, Saturday...) and dropped the monthly ones, which no weekday holds (only the seed's demo had rows; `week-plan-migration.spec.ts` (retired 2026-10-13)). Occasional evenings are planned by hand. One writer, `saveWeekTemplate` (replaced whole; the changes feed's `weekTemplate` through 0052's stamp, `weekTemplatesByUserIds`, #716); one form, `WeekTemplateBody`/`readWeekTemplateForm` (`template.ts`), which the seed posts through too. The editor is its own page, Profile › Your week (`GET /auth/profile/week`, `WeekTemplatePage`, `views.tsx`; a Profile section until #728, whose `#week` row now opens it and shows `weekTemplateSummary`): per weekday a select for the day's outfit and chips for the rest, **one Save** (a native `PostForm` to the same address, 303 to `/auth/profile/week?saved=1`), not save-on-change: seven rows are one decision, and a half-edited week must not be planned from.
- **Days away are not planned** (#684): `planMyWeek` drops the days a trip covers (`splitAwayDays`, `src/web/trips/away.ts`) before `emptySlots`, answers them as `away` (the API's and `plan_week`'s too), and the banner says "Not planned while you are away: ..." (`data-away-skipped`), never "Still empty"; a trip's create and any change of its dates remove the unworn auto entries on its days from today on in their own transaction (`createTripAt`, `updateTrip`; a past day's plan stays, still the person's to mark worn), and the re-plan removes any left there (a trip written by the seed or a fixture), all through `removeAutoEntries` (by ids or a day range; Undo's rule: the outfit it made goes once unheld), the re-plan's logged `..., N removed from days away`, never pushed; a person's or an agent's entry there stays.
- **The planner** (`planWeek`, pure, `week-planner.spec.ts`): the template's slots of the days asked (today and the six after it), in day and occasion order; a slot is **empty** when no entry has its occasion that day, and a day occasion's slot when no day occasion is planned (one outfit dresses the day); today's slots whose window (`OCCASION_HINTS`) has ended are left. Each slot's idea is `generateIdeas` over the planner's pool with that slot's `weatherNeeds` (the occasion's window of that day's forecast, the personal offset) and the occasion's demands (`occasionDemands`), seeded by (day, occasion) (`slotSeed`, `PLANNER_VERSION`); the best idea is taken, a near miss included (its problems are reported); a slot with none stays empty (`unfilled`), as a workout does without clean activewear (#647: never a near miss; the banner says why, `data-needs-activewear`: `no-activewear` when the closet has none of the kinds, else `none-available` (it reads the calendar and the closet's kinds, `closetKindsSql`, not the plan's reasons)). **Wash limits across the week**: the pool is every closet garment not away, dirty ones too (`weekPoolSql`, beside `ideaPoolSql`), with its wash state today; the unworn entries in the window and every outfit planned so far add their wear **days**, and a garment is offered only while `cleanCopies` stays above zero after all of them, so a planned wear never dirties a copy a later planned day relies on (a garment already worn that day costs nothing more). Nothing is washed in between. **No outfit twice**: each planned outfit joins the saved outfits the generator never repeats. Rotation: a garment the week already wears counts as just worn for later slots.
- **Rain gear is the web layer's read** (#759): `weekRainGear` (`plan.ts`, over `rainGearFor`) reads the closet's gear once per plan or re-plan run, only when a slot being planned or judged asks for water resistance, and hands it to the pure planner as `WeekContext.rainGear`; the planner never scans its pool for it, so a hat answers a rainy day here as on Today.
- **Writes** (`plan.ts`, `planMyWeek`): the forecast is read first (`weekForecast`, outside any lock), then one transaction under `lockOwner`: the template, the window's entries (`windowEntriesSql`) and its trips (one statement, #684), the pool, saved outfits and avoided pairs are read **after** the lock, so a double tap or a retried `plan_week` waits and finds its slots filled (`/calendar?planned=none`); with no empty slot (`emptySlots`, the slots `planWeek` fills) it stops before the pool and the memory (#165, #684: 5 statements with the session). Each outfit goes through **`pickIdea`** (the gallery's pick: reused or created and named `ideaName`) with `plannedBy: 'auto'`, its entry recorded in the batch. Tables: `week_plan` (the batch: one tap, for the banner and Undo) and `week_plan_entry` (entry, batch, whether the planner created the outfit, and the targets it was planned for: `torso`, `limbs`, `layer`, `rain`, all null without a forecast, and `limbs_ceiling`, null also for entries from before 0054).
- **`outfit_calendar.planned_by`** (`auto` | `agent` | `user` in rank order, default and every entry before #16 `user`; `agent` since #682, with `planned_by_token_id`): who owns the choice. Its values are written only through `src/web/calendar/planned-by.ts` (`chooserOf`, `takeOverSql`, `PERSONS_CHOICE`): re-choosing the same outfit takes an entry over only from a lower rank, a replace makes it the chooser's, the person's edits and wears make it `user`; only `auto` is ever re-planned. **Every writer that edits an entry makes it the person's**: `setEntryWorn` (marking or unmarking), `updateOutfit` (the calendar chip's edit link is the outfit form; every auto entry of the outfit), `setEntryOutfit` (Change, #69, which also removes the planner's outfit it replaced once unheld, through Undo's `removeUnheldOutfits`) and `insertEntry` when the person plans the outfit already auto on that day (the outfit page's Plan, the plan page's and the Saved tab's pick, `schedule_outfit`, a pick with that day: #77, see Calendar). **A person's pick or save of a planner-made outfit's garments makes the outfit theirs** (`adoptOutfit`, called by `createOutfit`'s reuse, which also raises the outfit's `origin`, #659; see Outfits): its entries stay the planner's, but Undo, a swap and Change no longer delete it (`outfit_created` is what `removeAutoEntries` passes to `removeUnheldOutfits`; `outfitIsHeld` is not widened: it answers "what references this outfit", a different question). Only `auto` entries are ever re-planned or undone. The row carries an Auto badge (`OccasionRow`, `data-auto`), `get_calendar` its `plannedBy`.
- **Undo** (`undoWeekPlan`, under `lockOwner`): the batch's entries still `auto` are deleted with each outfit the planner created for them that nothing holds any more (`removeBatchAutoEntries`, one `DELETE ... USING` scoped to the owner's batch, then `removeUnheldOutfits`: no calendar entry and no trip, `outfitIsHeld`); entries taken over since stay; the batch goes once empty. Whose batch it is is asked only when the delete found nothing (another's, whatever share, is `NotFound('weekPlan')` from `undoWeekPlan(db, actor, id)` itself, #188d; an own one fully taken over `undone=0`). **`removeUnheldOutfits` is two statements whatever the count** (#165; Undo, the swap, Change and the calendar's x, `deleteEntry`, #659, #748, share it; it deletes only origin `idea`/`planner` outfits, so a `person` or `agent` one is safe whatever the caller passes): the outfits locked, then one delete of the unheld ones, not `deleteOutfit` per outfit (4 each): an unheld outfit has no entry, wear, selfie or trip for its rules to keep. Never fold the two into one: a delete waiting on the row lock judges `outfitIsHeld` by its own older snapshot. Undo is 8 statements (`week-plan.spec.ts`).
- **The daily re-plan** (`replan.ts`): `replanToday(deps, userId, now)` is one user's re-plan for the day, **once, whoever asks**: the minutely run (`replanWeeks(deps, now)`, every user `replanCandidates` lists: an auto entry recorded in a batch from today on and no claim today) and **the morning reminder, which re-plans its person first** (#76, see Web Push). Scheduled by `server.ts` only (every minute through `scheduleMinutely`, with or without `WEATHER_ENABLED`: without a forecast it still judges availability; claims pruned nightly), never by `createApp()`, so the harness and the CLIs never re-plan (a spec calls it with the instant it wants). The run does nothing before **`REPLAN_HOUR`, derived from `REMINDER_WINDOWS.morning.from`** (05:00 on the household's wall clock, `hourIn`): everyone is re-planned by the earliest time a reminder can be set to, and a swap notice comes before a reminder; the reminder does not depend on it. **The claim commits with the work**: per user one transaction under `lockOwner` that reads the auto entries and claims the day in one statement (`claimAutoEntries`: a data-modifying CTE inserting into `week_replan (user_id, day)`, `ON CONFLICT DO NOTHING`, only when an auto entry exists, so a week planned later today is judged by a later run), judges and writes. A second caller (the next minute, the reminder, a second server in an overlapping deploy) waits on the owner lock and finds the day claimed and the re-plan committed, never half done, which is what lets a reminder read the day after it. A re-plan that throws is logged and its day left unclaimed (the claim rolled back with the work); **`ReplanRetries`** (in process, held by `server.ts`'s one `ReplanDeps`, so the minutely run and the morning reminder share it; no table, a restart only retries sooner) retries that person after 5, 15 and 60 minutes, and the fourth failure claims the day (`claimAfterFailure`), so a persistent failure is four attempts a day, not one a minute. Each job counts only its own first failure of the person-day into its `run.failed` (`job`: `replan` or `reminders`; the reminder's must not use up the minutely run's, #858), so each scheduled run ends with one `PartialFailure` per person-day after re-planning the others (`replanWeeksWhole`, #687: one `failure` metric and one capture). Without `retries` (specs) a failure claims the day at once. **A forecast fetch that fails with nothing cached is no forecast, not a failure** (`userWeatherOrNone`: the re-plan judges availability, the reminder drops its weather line); one that timed out waiting on the owner lock (`OwnerLockTimeout`, see Calendar) judged nothing and is logged `deferred`, the day left unclaimed for the next minute and not a failure; the swap notice goes after the commit (a crash between loses the notice, never doubles it). The judgement (`replanWeek`, pure): **an outfit that can no longer be worn** (`unwearableOn`, `isAvailable` on the planned day's terms: a garment archived or a wishlist item, lent or at repair, deleted since (an empty slot, only telling in an outfit the planner created: every slot had a garment; a reused saved outfit may have one empty on purpose), or, **on today only**, without a clean copy and not worn today) is swapped for the slot's best idea under the planner's rules, whatever the forecast, and kept (logged) when nothing else dresses the slot. Dirty counts only on the day itself because today's wash state is a fact and a later day's a projection the next load of laundry changes; the run is daily, so a garment still dirty on its day is caught that morning, before the person dresses (the planner's own "nothing is washed in between" is right for filling a week and too eager for undoing one). Otherwise the weather: an entry whose targets (torso, limbs, layer, rain) did not change, **and whose legs and feet did not turn too warm** (the stored `limbs` is capped on the cold side and cannot show `limbsCeiling`, the uncapped too-warm bound, so `week_plan_entry.limbs_ceiling` keeps it: warmth-5 boots planned for the cold; judged only when `assessOutfit` finds legs or feet too warm now and the ceiling is lower than the one it was planned with, so a near miss the planner chose knowingly is not re-judged in unchanged weather, and an entry with a null ceiling, planned before 0054, never is; a fallen ceiling is the one cause, so such a swap says "warmer"; a kept entry records the new ceiling), or whose day has no forecast is kept; with changed targets an outfit that still fits them is kept (recording the new targets), else the slot is planned again and **swapped only for an idea that answers the weather better** (the old entry and its planner-made outfit removed, the new one in the same batch). What swapped is **pushed** to the person's devices **with the morning reminder on**, one line per swap saying why ("Thursday turned cold: swapped in Wool coat", "Bean Boots is at repair: swapped in Chelsea boots on Thursday", "A garment of Friday's outfit was deleted: swapped in ..."; title "Your week changed", opens `/calendar`, tag `week-replan`, TTL 6 h): the notice is about what to wear, the reminder's subject, so no new setting; without `PWA_ENABLED` swaps happen and nobody is told. **One push per swap, never two**: when the morning reminder runs the re-plan (`replanToday(..., { announcesToday: true })`), today's swaps are said in the reminder itself and left out of the notice, which then goes only for other days' swaps (if any); the minutely run announces everything, today included. `replanToday` returns every swap it made either way. `outfitGarmentStatesSql` (`queries.ts`) reads the auto entries' outfits' slots with each garment's status, away, wash state (`wearsSinceWashSql`) and whether it was worn today. **The minutely run's statements** (#165, #173: 2 + 5 per due user, 7 for one; `week-plan.spec.ts`, `job-statements.spec.ts`): the candidates, every candidate's weather in one statement (`refreshForecastsFor` answers it, so `replanUser` does not read it again), then per user the transaction: begin, the owner lock, `claimAutoEntries`, then the window, the pool, the memory, the outfits' garments and the trips in one `selectScalars` (`weekSql` + `outfitGarmentStatesSql` + `awayTripsSql`, each a scalar subquery: `windowEntriesSql`, `weekPoolSql`, `generatorMemorySql`), commit (a removal from a day away adds its own statements); swap notices go last, in one `sendEach` for the run. A listed user is not asked `replanCandidates` again; the morning reminders ask it once for all their people and pass their batch's weather (`replanToday`'s `listed`, `weather`). "Plan my week" reads its pool and memory in one statement too.
- **Routes** (`routes.tsx`; the API's twins are `api-routes.ts`, #193b: `POST /week-plans`, `DELETE /week-plans/{id}`, `GET`/`PUT /week-template`; the button, `plan_week` and `POST /week-plans` all plan through `planMyWeekNow`): `POST /calendar/plan-week` (no input; no template: 303 to `/auth/profile/week`; else 303 to `/calendar?week=<Sunday of the batch's first planned day>&planned=<batch>#day-<first>` (#683: Sunday-to-Saturday is the page's window, "today and six" the plan's, so the first planned day may be next week), or `/calendar?planned=none`), `POST /calendar/plan-week/:id/undo` (303 to `/calendar?undone=N`; another's: 404), `GET /auth/profile/week` (the template's page; `?saved=1` its toast) and `POST /auth/profile/week` (`WeekTemplateBody`; an occasion outside its field's set is a 400). The calendar reads `?planned=` (the banner: the batch's entries, each row a link to its day with a `thumb` collage (`#day-D` when in the shown week, else `dayUrl`; `batchEntriesSql` reads the garments), the template slots still empty, Undo; `plannedBanner` from `batchEntriesSql` and `entrySlotsSql`, read in the week's one statement, `weekContext`) and `?undone=` (a toast) as navigation state, both stripped from the address (`StripFlags`); the "Plan my week" button is static, so `/calendar` stays byte-stable. Today shows the same button.

## Gotchas

- **An auto entry is the planner's only until someone touches it.** `outfit_calendar.planned_by` is what keeps the daily re-plan and Undo off a person's choice (#16): a new writer that edits an entry (its outfit, its occasion, worn) must set it to `user` in the same transaction, as `setEntryWorn`, `updateOutfit` and `setEntryOutfit` do, or the next morning's re-plan may swap out what they chose. **Choosing what the planner chose is touching it**: `insertEntry` adopts an `auto` entry the person plans again (`ON CONFLICT ... DO UPDATE ... WHERE`), and `createOutfit` (every pick and save) adopts a planner-made outfit a person's save reuses (`adoptOutfit`, `adopt.ts`); a new path where the person picks or plans something that may already be the planner's goes through them, never around (an `ON CONFLICT DO NOTHING` of its own left #77's entries `auto`). Only the planner writes `auto` (through `pickIdea`'s `plannedBy`), and a new reader of "the planner's entries" filters `planned_by = 'auto'` rather than joining `week_plan_entry` alone (that row stays, as provenance, after the person takes the entry over).
- **Op lines** (#712, `docs/conventions.md#logging`): the writes log once from `plan-actions.ts` (`logOp`, whichever door: the button, `POST /week-plans`, `plan_week`): `week.planned` (`changed` false when nothing was planned: no template, `templateSet` false, or every slot filled; `weekPlanId`, `planned`, `entries`, `unfilled`, `weatherDays`, `ms`), `week.plan.undone` and `week.template.saved`. The daily re-plan's job lines above are unchanged.
