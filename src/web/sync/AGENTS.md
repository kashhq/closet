# The changes feed

`GET /api/v1/changes?cursor=&limit=` (#196b; `listChanges`): a native client's sync, every synced row and deletion after its cursor, across the caller's own wardrobe and each one shared with them. No page twin: the PWA reads pages. The rows are stamped by the sync triggers (`src/db/AGENTS.md`, The sync triggers); the plan is PR #605.

- **Files**: `feed.ts` (`readChanges`, the registry `FEED`), `coverage.ts` (`READ_COVERAGE`, below), `cursor.ts` (the position, its encoding, `isExpired`), `queries.ts` (the wardrobes read, the keyset read), `api-routes.ts`.
- **Coverage is declared** (#716): `READ_COVERAGE` sorts every `/api/v1` GET into feed-backed (the feed resources a client derives it from) or online only (with why), rendered into `listChanges`' description as a table; `coverage.spec.ts` (unit) fails on a GET of the committed `openapi.json` in no row or two, and a `FEED` name in none. A new GET route gets its row in the same PR. Today:

  | Feed-backed | Online only |
  | --- | --- |
  | garments, wears, outfits, the calendar and selfies, week-plan batches, the week template, trips, capsules, sizes and measurements, the style profile with its rules | Today (forecast, suggestions), ideas, clashes and avoided pairs, the wishlist's needs and Muse inbox, looks (owner decision), selfie and look photos, insights and the recap, weather and a trip's forecast, shared wardrobes, `getMe`, the spec |
- **One `REPEATABLE READ READ ONLY` transaction per page**: the wardrobes (self, then each accepted share by owner id, `acceptedSharesWith`) with `revision` and `sync_floor`, then one keyset `union all` (a branch per resource and one for `sync_tombstone`, per wardrobe, each on its `(owner, revision)` index; a stamp's on the user row's key), then one batched read per resource on the page. 6 statements for a page of garments at any `limit`, 15 for a page of the ten resources its fixture writes (a read each), 5 for an empty page (`api-statements.spec.ts`). Never a per-row read.
- **Order within a wardrobe: `(revision, kind, resource, id, seq)`**: kind 0 a deletion, 1 a row (a revision's deletions first); `resource` the table, compared `COLLATE "C"` on both sides; `seq` the tombstone's id (0 for a row). A page fills one wardrobe before the next. Positions only move forward; a row rewritten between pages gets a higher revision and comes again (a resend, never a skip).
- **The end is exclusive** (`endOf`, kind 2): a finished wardrobe resumes after the snapshot's `user.revision`, so a wardrobe still at the backfill's 0 is not resent forever. The end comes from the snapshot, never the highest row seen: a revision commits with its rows under the owner lock, so nothing at or below it is in flight.
- **Expiry** (`isExpired`): a position before the end of `sync_floor`'s deletions is a 410 `cursor_expired` (full sync), unless its pass began at or above the floor (`Position.floor`: a full sync paging through rows below the floor must not restart forever). A fresh read leaves out tombstones at or below the floor. So does a cursor answered when `SYNC_ROOTS` or `SYNC_STAMPS` was shorter (`FEED_COVERAGE`, a bit per root, then per stamp): it never saw the new resources' older rows. #608 moved it once, and #716's stamp bit (the week template) again: every device's next call is a 410 `cursor_expired` and a full re-sync after that deploy (intended; it also gives cached profiles their `rules`). So does one answered to another user (`u`, #617: a device that switched accounts) or naming none (answered before #617): its positions are another account's, never trusted. So does one of another epoch (`e`, #652: `sync_epoch`'s random uuid, left-joined onto the wardrobes statement's rows, so no statement more; no row is `SyncEpochMissingError`, a 500, never read as a missing user), which `bumpSyncEpoch` (`src/db/sync-epoch.ts`) replaces after a restore: a restored database reuses revisions and ids the device already holds. A cursor without `e` (answered before #652) is current until the first bump (`isCurrentEpoch`: nothing was restored before, so that deploy re-syncs no one). And one whose position is above its wardrobe's current revision (a restore the epoch missed). A cursor that fails the schema stays a 400. **Cursor JSON**: `{ v: 1, u, e, c, s?, w: { <owner>: [revision, kind, resource, id, seq, floor] } }`.
- **The floor moves only by the prune** (`src/maintenance/sync-prune.ts`, nightly `sync_prune`, #609): a tombstone without a client id past 90 days by Postgres's `now()` is deleted and its owner's `sync_floor` raised to the newest revision pruned in the same statement (owners locked in id order, batches of 1000), so a page's snapshot sees both or neither. A client id's tombstone is never pruned (it refuses a replayed create, #607); below the floor it is sent to no one.
- **A full sync is self-describing** (`snapshot`): every page of a sync begun without a cursor carries `snapshot: { wardrobes }` (the caller's own, then each accepted share), held across pages by the cursor's `s: 1` until the page where `more` is false. After that page the client drops every synced row not re-sent and every wardrobe not listed, since the fresh read sends no deletions at or below the floor and no `revokedWardrobes`. A 410 mid-way restarts it from no cursor.
- **A grantee's wardrobe** carries only `shared` entries (garments, every status, and capsules), its garments in the grantee shape (no `care`, no `repairs`: `garmentsByIds` reads the log only where the viewer owns it). A cursor naming an owner the caller no longer sees drops it and lists it in `revokedWardrobes`. `?ownerId=` is a 400 (the closed query).
- **A feed shape is the API resource minus what tables that never move its revision derive** (decisions 2026-11-13); the client derives those from the feed's other resources. Per resource (`Synced…` beside its API resource):
  - `garment`: no `worn`, `wornSinceWash`, `lastWorn`, `cost`, `costPerWear`, `capsules` (wears, capsules).
  - `outfit`: `slots` (category, `garmentId`) for `garments` and `toBuy` (the garments'); no proposal `agent` (the token's name), `planned` or `worn` (the entries'). Muse's proposals are in it, with their reaction (decided on #607). `origin` (#659): migration 0053 re-sent only the rows it moved off `person`, so **a cached outfit without `origin` is `person`**.
  - `calendarEntry`: `outfitId` for `outfit` (its name and garments); no `selfieId`/`selfiePhoto` (the `selfie` names its entry).
  - `trip`: its stored fields, `outfits` flat (for `days`/`anyDay`), `extras`, `packedGarmentIds` (stale marks included: the reads ignore them); no `phase` (the day's), outfit garments, `wornTodayEntryId` (the calendar's) or `packing`, derived from the outfits' slots and garments.
  - `weekPlan` (no API resource): `createdAt`, `entries` (`entryId`, `outfitCreated`); not the planner's targets, the re-plan's own input.
  - `selfie`, `brandSize`, `measurements`: nothing dropped.
  - `styleProfile`: its fields and `rules` (`SyncedStyleProfile`, #716), each rule's sides without `label` or `sentence` (a garment's name: its rename never moves the profile). `outfit_rule` is the profile's child (`createsParent`: a rule before any profile creates the row), so every rule write, a garment delete's cascade included, re-sends the profile.
  - `weekTemplate` (#716, the API's `WeekTemplate`): a **stamp**, not a root (`SYNC_STAMPS`): no row of its own, its revision `user.week_template_revision` (0052's trigger), read from the user row. Every user has one: never set, it is seven empty days at revision 0, so a full sync carries it; emptied, a change, never a deletion (no tombstone).
  - `styleProfile`, `measurements`, `weekTemplate` are one per user, the change's `id` the user's; all owner-only.
- **Adding a resource**: a new `SYNC_ROOTS` table or `SYNC_STAMPS` entry fails tsc until `FEED` has its entry, and `coverage.spec.ts` until a `READ_COVERAGE` row names it (a `Record` of `SyncResource`): its API `name`, `shared` (`feed.spec.ts` holds it to garments and capsules, the share-aware list), its titled feed schema beside its API resource, and a batched `…ByIds(db, ids)` reader beside its per-id one, one statement for any owners, in its feature's `queries.ts`. Regenerate `openapi.json`, extend `api-changes.spec.ts` and the statements pin.
- **Tests**: `test/integration/api-changes.spec.ts` (the delta across pages, a page boundary inside one revision, the backfill, a held writer, the floor, a full sync's snapshot, a grantee's feed; per owner resource group its delta, a child write resending its parent, the week template and rules across devices); `feed.spec.ts` (the registry, `isExpired`); `coverage.spec.ts` (the declared coverage); `sync-prune.spec.ts` (what the prune keeps, the floor against cursors, a prune committing under a held page).
