# The changes feed

_Reference, not auto-loaded:_ [`reference.md`](reference.md) holds A feed shape is the API resource min...; Tests.

`GET /api/v1/changes?cursor=&limit=` (#196b; `listChanges`): a native client's sync, every synced row and deletion after its cursor, across the caller's own wardrobe and each one shared with them. No page twin: the PWA reads pages. The rows are stamped by the sync triggers (`src/db/AGENTS.md`, The sync triggers); the plan is PR #605.

- **Files**: `feed.ts` (`readChanges`, the registry `FEED`), `coverage.ts` (`READ_COVERAGE`, below), `cursor.ts` (the position, its encoding, `isExpired`), `queries.ts` (the wardrobes read, the keyset read), `api-routes.ts`.
- **Coverage is declared** (#716): `READ_COVERAGE` sorts every `/api/v1` GET into feed-backed (the feed resources a client derives it from) or online only (with why), rendered into `listChanges`' description as a table; `coverage.spec.ts` (unit) fails on a GET of the committed `openapi.json` in no row or two, and a `FEED` name in none. A new GET route gets its row in the same PR. Today:
  | Feed-backed | Online only |
  | --- | --- |
  | garments, wears, outfits, the calendar and selfies, week-plan batches, the week template, trips, capsules, sizes and measurements, the style profile with its rules | Today (forecast, suggestions), ideas, clashes and avoided pairs, the wishlist's needs and Muse inbox, looks (owner decision), selfie and look photos, insights and the recap, weather and a trip's forecast, shared wardrobes, `getMe`, the spec |
- **One `REPEATABLE READ READ ONLY` transaction per page**: the wardrobes (self, then each accepted share by owner id, `acceptedSharesWith`) with `revision` and `sync_floor`, then one keyset `union all` (a branch per resource and one for `sync_tombstone`, per wardrobe, each on its `(owner, revision)` index; a stamp's on the user row's key), then one batched read per resource on the page. The count does not grow with `limit` or the rows (`api-statements.spec.ts`): never a per-row read.
- **Order within a wardrobe: `(revision, kind, resource, id, seq)`**: kind 0 a deletion, 1 a row (a revision's deletions first); `resource` the table, compared `COLLATE "C"` on both sides; `seq` the tombstone's id (0 for a row). A page fills one wardrobe before the next. Positions only move forward; a row rewritten between pages gets a higher revision and comes again (a resend, never a skip).
- **The end is exclusive** (`endOf`, kind 2): a finished wardrobe resumes after the snapshot's `user.revision`, so a wardrobe still at the backfill's 0 is not resent forever. The end comes from the snapshot, never the highest row seen: a revision commits with its rows under the owner lock, so nothing at or below it is in flight.
- **Expiry** (`isExpired`): a position before the end of `sync_floor`'s deletions is a 410 `cursor_expired` (full sync), unless its pass began at or above the floor (`Position.floor`: a full sync paging through rows below the floor must not restart forever). A fresh read leaves out tombstones at or below the floor. So does a cursor answered when `SYNC_ROOTS` or `SYNC_STAMPS` was shorter (`FEED_COVERAGE`, a bit per root, then per stamp): it never saw the new resources' older rows. #608 moved it once, and #716's stamp bit (the week template) again: every device's next call is a 410 `cursor_expired` and a full re-sync after that deploy (intended; it also gives cached profiles their `rules`). So does one answered to another user (`u`, #617: a device that switched accounts) or naming none (answered before #617): its positions are another account's, never trusted. So does one of another epoch (`e`, #652: `sync_epoch`'s random uuid, left-joined onto the wardrobes statement's rows, so no statement more; no row is `SyncEpochMissingError`, a 500, never read as a missing user), which `bumpSyncEpoch` (`src/db/sync-epoch.ts`) replaces after a restore: a restored database reuses revisions and ids the device already holds. A cursor without `e` (answered before #652) is current until the first bump (`isCurrentEpoch`: nothing was restored before, so that deploy re-syncs no one). And one whose position is above its wardrobe's current revision (a restore the epoch missed). A cursor that fails the schema stays a 400. **Cursor JSON**: `{ v: 1, u, e, c, s?, w: { <owner>: [revision, kind, resource, id, seq, floor] }, g: { <owner>: 'OWN' | 'VIEW' | 'MANAGE' } }`.
- **The floor moves only by the prune** (`src/maintenance/sync-prune.ts`, nightly `sync_prune`, #609): a tombstone without a client id past 90 days by Postgres's `now()` is deleted and its owner's `sync_floor` raised to the newest revision pruned in the same statement (owners locked in id order, batches of 1000), so a page's snapshot sees both or neither. A client id's tombstone is never pruned (it refuses a replayed create, #607); below the floor it is sent to no one.
- **A full sync is self-describing** (`snapshot`): every page of a sync begun without a cursor carries `snapshot: { wardrobes }` (the caller's own, then each accepted share), held across pages by the cursor's `s: 1` until the page where `more` is false. After that page the client drops every synced row not re-sent and every wardrobe not listed, since the fresh read sends no deletions at or below the floor and no `revokedWardrobes`. A 410 mid-way restarts it from no cursor.
- **A grantee's wardrobe** carries only `shared` entries (garments, every status, and capsules), its garments in the grantee shape (no `care`, no `repairs`: `garmentsByIds` reads the log only where the viewer owns it; no `price` or `notes` on a VIEW share, `privateColumnsWhere`, #680). A cursor naming an owner the caller no longer sees drops it and lists it in `revokedWardrobes`. **A grant is part of the position** (#959): a wardrobe whose grant differs from the cursor's `g` (a share raised to MANAGE, or revoked and given again) is read again from its start, its old position still checked for expiry: a share write moves no owner revision, yet a garment's `publicShareUrl` follows the grant. A cursor without `g` resends every wardrobe once. `?ownerId=` is a 400 (the closed query).
- **Adding a resource**: a new `SYNC_ROOTS` table or `SYNC_STAMPS` entry fails tsc until `FEED` has its entry (a `Record` of `SyncResource`), and `coverage.spec.ts` until a `READ_COVERAGE` row names it: its API `name`, `shared` (`feed.spec.ts` holds it to garments and capsules, the share-aware list), its titled feed schema beside its API resource, and a batched `…ByIds(db, ids)` reader beside its per-id one, one statement for any owners, in its feature's `queries.ts`. Regenerate `openapi.json`, extend `api-changes.spec.ts` and the statements pin.
- **Moved rules** (detail in `reference.md`): a feed shape leaves out what tables that never move its revision derive (a style rule's garment `label`); the client derives it. A cached outfit without `origin` is `person`. `weekTemplate` always exists (seven empty days at revision 0), and emptying it is a change, never a deletion.
