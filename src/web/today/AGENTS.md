# Today

`src/web/today/` (#15; plan section 9, redesign "Today"): `GET /`, the home screen, the dock's first tab and the manifest's `start_url` (its `id` stays `/wardrobe`: the installed app's identity). The signed-in user's own day, like the calendar; signed out it is the login redirect like any page. **Today stores nothing of its own**: it reads the calendar and the generator and writes through their writers.

- **The model is `todayFor`** (`today.ts`), shared by the page, the MCP tool `get_today` and the morning push reminder (which hands it the batch's weather, `ownWeather`, #173), so the three never disagree: a row per occasion planned today (`entriesSql`, shared with the calendar: occasion order, each entry with its outfit's collage garments), and, **while nothing that dresses the day is planned** (`DAY_OCCASIONS` in `src/wardrobe/occasions.ts`: all day, work, daytime), an all-day row of suggestions first: `todayIdeas`, `ideasFor` for today's forecast with the day's seed (`dailySeed`, as the gallery opens), a page of `TODAY_IDEAS` (3), and the person's weather (`weatherWithForecastSql` then `userWeatherFrom`, null with the weather off), which the line and the ideas share. A day with only a run and a dinner planned still gets ideas for what to wear between; a work day does not. Worn today (`somethingWornSql`: a worn entry or a garment's "Wore today") is read only when asked (`todayFor`'s `worn`, in the same statement), since the page never shows it: `get_today` asks, and the evening reminder reads it with the day's bare entries (`entriesOfDaySql`) for every evening person in one statement (`eveningDays`, #173): the evening names the plan and needs neither weather nor ideas, so it never builds the model.
- **The page** (`today-page.tsx`): the day's date, the weather line (`UserWeatherLine`, rendered in the page: see Reads), "Away: <place>" linking to the trip on a trip day (`AwayLink`; `todayFor`'s `away`, `awayTripsSql` in `readDay`'s statement, #684; the rows are unchanged), **one Muse card**: Muse's latest round while any of it waits ("Muse: 3 outfits, 7 pieces to consider", its one-line summary, Review to `/outfits`, or the Wishlist inbox when it brought no outfit; `museRoundSql`, `src/web/wishlist/rounds.ts`, #337: counted live, so it shrinks as the owner decides and goes at zero), else while an open need has an option to choose ("Muse has 4 needs for you to decide on", Review to the Wishlist inbox; `needsToDecideSql`, #333); neither has anything to dismiss (the plans' next-step card went with plans, #337, D9: Ready to buy is the inbox's version), then the rows. A planned entry is a card (`OutfitCollage` with `entryPieces`: a lent, at-repair, archived or unwashed piece wears its mark and the link's name says it, #358; Change is the way out, nothing is swapped; the name) with **"Wore it"** (`WornControl size="button"`, a native post to the calendar's `POST /calendar/:id/worn` with `returnTo=/`: one worn write wherever it is tapped; native, not in place, because the card's Change goes once worn) and **"Change"** (the gallery with `?for=day:<today>&occasion=&replace=<entry>`: the pick takes the entry's place, `replaceEntryOutfit`, #69; not offered once worn); worn, "✓ Worn" and Undo (`worn=0`). An ideas row is the day's advice ("Take the umbrella", `adviceChips`, once per row) and a scroll-snap strip of cards (collage, name, `Reasons`, **"Wear this"**), **Refresh** (`GET /today/ideas?occasion=&page=`, the next page swapped over the row, `hx-target="closest [data-today-row]"`; after the last page, the first again; lenient: malformed is all day, page 1) and "More ideas" (the gallery for today). "+ Plan another outfit" opens the plan page for today. **From lg** (#1065, one markup): each row is its heading with Refresh beside it, then its cards on `IDEA_GRID`'s tracks (the ideas strip's `desktop="grid"`, the planned cards' wrapper), rows stacked, never side by side (`PAIR_GRID` is gone here); "Plan my week" sits beside the weather line (`WeatherRow`: rendered there and in the foot, CSS showing one, so neither size's focus order differs from what it sees; `max-lg:contents` keeps the phone's boxes as they were).
- **The first-run path** (#666): `todayFor`'s `firstRun` (asked by the page only) reads `closetKindsSql` in `readDay`'s statement and judges it with `closetCanDress` (`src/web/gallery/queries.ts`, over `canProduceOutfit` in `src/wardrobe/properties.ts`: a top and a bottom, or a dress), the one gate Calendar's "Plan my week" and Styling's Include picks (`StripsReads.canDress`) use too, each in its own statement. Until it holds the page is `FirstRunCard` (`data-first-run="empty"`: the welcome and "Add your first garment"; `"incomplete"`: what is missing and "Add a garment"), its one primary button opening Wardrobe's own `AddSheet` in place (`sheetOpener(ADD_SHEET_ID)`, camera first); the weather line (the city) only once a garment is in; no "Plan another outfit", no "Plan my week" and no ideas row (none could be drawn: 2 statements). `first-run.spec.ts`.
- **"Wear this" is one transaction, and happens once** (`wearIdea`, `queries.ts`; `POST /today/wear`: `garmentId` × n and the `occasion`, a native `PostForm`, 303 back to `/`): `pickIdea` (under `lockOwner`: the outfit of exactly these garments, reused or created), then `wearOutfitOn` (planned today for the occasion through `insertEntry`, the entry marked worn through `setEntryWorn`), in the same transaction. A double tap's second transaction waits on the owner lock, finds the outfit and the entry, and `setEntryWorn` finds it worn (`changed: false`, logged "already worn"); `today-home.spec.ts` holds one transaction open while a second waits. Garments not in the owner's closet are a 404 with nothing written; a malformed post a 400. The API's twin is `POST /api/v1/outfit-wears` (`api-routes.ts`, #193b): the same `wearIdea`, but with the `day` sent (`wearIdea`'s `day` apart from `today`), judged by the queued taps' window. **The API's read** is `GET /api/v1/today` (`api-routes.ts`, `today-resource.ts`, #195a): `todayFor` with the page's `needs`, so the same 3 statements; an ideas row's `nextCursor` is Refresh as `GET /api/v1/ideas?limit=3&cursor=` (the same seeded read: page 1 here, then the cursor's offset).
- **Reads** (#158; production pays a ~114 ms round trip per statement, so count statements, not milliseconds; `today-home.spec.ts` and `weather.spec.ts` assert each count): **`GET /`** is the session, then the day's entries with the settings and forecast row and Muse's waiting needs and round in one statement (`readDay`: `entriesSql`, `weatherWithForecastSql` and, asked by the page only, `needsToDecideSql` and `museRoundSql` through `selectScalars`, #172, #333, #337; two in parallel before), then, while the day is undressed, `ideasFor`'s one statement (the pool, saved outfits and clashes, #168): 3 with the weather on or off, 2 dressed (or on the first-run path). A forecast that must be fetched (a location's first ask, a stale row) adds the cache's own statements. The line is rendered in the page from that weather read, not loaded after it as a `WeatherSlot` (the stale-while-revalidate pages must stay byte-stable; Today, network first, need not): one request and three statements fewer per open. The price: on a dressed day the page waits on a cold forecast miss (a location's first ask) that only the fragment waited on before. **Refresh** is the session, then the pool and the memory with the weather row in one statement (2). **"Wear this"** takes the owner lock once (`wearIdea` is the `ownerTransaction`; `pickIdea`, `insertEntry` and `setEntryWorn` join it, see Calendar's Owner lock): 9 on a repeat tap (the garments and the outfit they already are in one, #168), 25 before. **"Wore it"** is the calendar's `setEntryWorn` (6: the lock and its timeout are one statement, the worn mark and its wears another, #165).
- **Network first, not a stale-while-revalidate tab root** (decided in #15; `OPENS_STALE`, `src/web/page-cache.ts`): all of Today is the day's own data (the plan, the ideas, what was worn), so a byte-stable page would be a shell with a date and every row a fragment, and a copy opened first would show yesterday's plan with live "Wear this" buttons. NetworkFirst with the navigation preload answers a cold open in one round trip; offline the worker's last copy shows with its age (freshness.js) and its writes disabled (`data-needs-network`, a `data-offline-note`). `SECTION_HOME.today` is `/` (the dock's link) and is deliberately absent from the stale tab roots.
- **The dock** (`DOCK_TABS`, `src/web/layout/sections.ts`): Today · Wardrobe · Style · Outfits · Calendar, the owner's decision on #43 (2026-09-27; no centre "+"). `/` stays Today.
- **Logs** (logger `Web`; #712): one `idea.worn` line from `today-actions.ts`'s `wearIdeaOn` (`day`, `occasion`, `outfitId`, `entryId`, `garmentIds`, `changed` false for a double tap, `alreadySaved`, `wears`), whichever door; the page's model at debug.
