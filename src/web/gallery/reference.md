# Outfit gallery: reference

Moved out of `AGENTS.md` (#723) so the auto-loaded doc keeps only invariants, gotchas and ownership. Not auto-loaded: read it before changing this area. Where it disagrees with the code or a spec, they win.

- **The generator** (`generateIdeas`, generic over the caller's garment type; `generator.spec.ts`): templates top + bottom + footwear or one-piece + footwear (footwear only when the closet has any; a workout only top + active bottom + active footwear, `ActiveDemand`, #647; an empty workout answers `emptyReason`, `activewearGap`: `no-activewear` or `none-available`, on the Ideas empty state, `GET /ideas` and `suggest_outfits`), a layer only when the weather asks (the best-scoring option, then no layer; among layers tied best, one by rotation weight in an order keyed by the seed and the base's places (never ids, so a reseed draws the same), `rotationOrder`, #646, never one global preference). **The pool is the caller's**: `ideaPoolSql` reads `availableGarment()` (in the closet, not away, a clean copy left) and `inCapsule`; the generator never widens it, draws only layer, one-piece, top, bottom and footwear, and lets locked garments (any role, clean or not) ride along. Rotation: each role is drawn weighted by days since its last wear (`1 + min(idle, 60) / 10`; never worn is the cap). **Hard rules** (never relaxed): at most two colours beyond the neutrals (`neutralFor(role, color)`, the one place: black, white, grey, beige and brown on anything, and **blue on bottoms** (owner's rule: jeans and navy chinos go with anything; a blue top or layer counts); a colour counts once however many garments carry it) and one patterned garment (anything but solid, or the colour `pattern`); no pair in `generator_avoid` and none of the owner's **outfit rules** (`outfit_rule`, #21: never A with B, always B with A, by garment, category and type, colour or pattern; `src/wardrobe/outfit-rules.ts`, the avoided pairs compiled into the same list as never-rules; a never-rule two locked garments break flags the idea `'breaks-rule'` rather than emptying the page, its chip naming each rule (`brokenRuleLabels`, Style profile's; only Styling's Shuffle locks two today, so Ideas and Today never show one); an always-rule nothing drawable answers is moot; Garment model's Gotchas); no combination equal to a saved outfit, compared on the drawn roles (a saved outfit with a belt still counts); every locked garment in every idea. **Soft rules, a score**: `assessOutfit` against the day's `weatherNeeds` (the occasion's window, the personal offset) plus one per formality step outside `OCCASION_HINTS`' range; ideas scoring 0 come first in draw order, then the near misses best first, each with its problems named (`IdeaProblem`). **Advice for the day is not a problem**: rain the umbrella answers (`WeatherNeeds.umbrella`, Weather's Matching; `ideasFrom` applies the closet's rain gear (`rainGearFor`, `src/web/weather/queries.ts`: the whole closet, never the capsule's pool, except for `for=trip:ID`, whose ideas carry `IdeasInput.tripId` and read the trip's packing list like its forecast does; one more read only on a day that asks for water resistance) through `withRainGear`, so the cards and the advice read one needs) is scored nothing and shown once above the list (`adviceChips`, `chips.tsx`: the Ideas header and Today's ideas row), never on a card.

- **`generator_avoid`** (`drizzle/0018_generator_avoid.sql`): `(owner_id, garment_a_id, garment_b_id)` primary key, `garment_a_id < garment_b_id` by a check, both garments and the owner cascade. One writer, `avoidPair` (both garments the owner's, owned now or once, locked `FOR SHARE`; `on conflict do nothing`: 'added', 'already' or 'not-found'), and `allowPair(db, actor, a, b)`, its undo (a pair the actor never avoided is `NotFound('avoidedPair')`, thrown there). The garment page lists its pairs ("Never paired with", `avoided.tsx`) and the Style page all of them (`avoidedPairsByNameSql`, #21b), each with "Allow again", owner only.
- **The page** (`ideas-page.tsx`): a `PageShell wide` page whose cards sit in the shared `SnapStrip` (`size="page"`: the strip's width on a phone, `listbox={false}` as the cards hold forms, `desktop="grid"`: from `lg` the cards are an `IDEA_GRID` that scrolls nothing, #1065; `#idea-strip`; the page renders `SnapStripsInit`): swiping is the browser's scrolling, no library or touch handlers (#321). Today's rows and Goes-with use the same strip (`peek`, a grid too; `pair`, or `page` for one, a `row` from `lg`). The last child is the sentinel, which the grid's last row brings in on a desktop (`hx-trigger="intersect once"`, `hx-swap="outerHTML"`; Gotchas). A card is the shared `OutfitCollage` (`src/web/outfits/collage.tsx`; sizes `card` (Ideas, Today, the outfit page), `tile` (the Saved grid) and `thumb` (the garment page, the calendar)), the name, its reasons (`Reasons`; the chips are `chips.tsx`, which Styling's fit line shares), the pick and "Not this" (too warm / too cold, "Not today" which only removes the card, the clash pairs). The header: day and occasion (back to the plan page), capsule menu, "With …" for `?with=`, weather, Shuffle. An unnamed garment is its category's label wherever named (`name ?? categoryLabel(category)`). The forecast is read only for days it covers (today to 15 days ahead); past it only a trip day has weather, its destination's typical day (see Trips). **Offline**: the cards the worker cached show; every write is `data-needs-network`; a `data-offline-note` explains.
- **Entry points**: the calendar's plan page ("Choose from ideas" with its `?for=`), a capsule's "Swipe outfits" (`?capsule=`, the owner's), the Saved tab. "Style this" opens Styling; the gallery's `?with=` stays for its links and `suggest_outfits`.
