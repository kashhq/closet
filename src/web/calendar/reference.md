# Calendar: reference

Moved out of `AGENTS.md` (#723) so the auto-loaded doc keeps only invariants, gotchas and ownership. Not auto-loaded: read it before changing this area. Where it disagrees with the code or a spec, they win.

- **Changing an entry's outfit**: `replaceEntryOutfit` (`replace.ts`), the one replace write, reached from POST /calendar's `replace`, the gallery's pick with `replace` and `schedule_outfit`'s `replaceEntryId`. `replaceEntryOutfit({ db, logger }, actor, target, choice, madeBy)` answers what it changed or throws its refusal with nothing written (#188d; no outcome union for callers to translate). One owner transaction: the entry (the owner's, on that day and, when given, that occasion; else 404) locked, then the outfit (`ownsOutfit`, or an idea via `pickIdea`), then `setEntryOutfit` (the entry keeps its id, day and occasion). Rules: **a worn entry is refused** (409: it records that day's wears; Change shows on unworn entries only); **its selfie is kept as a look on its day** (`detachEntrySelfie`); **the entry becomes the person's** (`planned_by` 'user'), also when the choice is the outfit it already has (an `auto` entry taken over, `adopted`); **an outfit "Plan my week" created for it** goes once nothing holds it (`removeUnheldOutfits`, `outfitIsHeld`), only while the entry was `auto`; **the new outfit already on that day** is refused (409, naming its occasion). Idempotent: a double tap's second request waits on the lock and finds `unchanged` ("Already saved"). Logs (logger `Web`): one `calendar.entry.changed` line after commit (`entryId`, `day`, `occasion`, `previousOutfitId`, `outfitId`, `changed` false for the outfit it already holds, `outfitCreated`, `adopted`, `selfieDetached`, `plannerOutfitsRemoved`); a refusal is the error line with its `logDetail`, `... -> 409: ... (Calendar entry <id> (<day> <occasion>) not changed for user <id>: <reason>)`. `test/integration/replace-entry.spec.ts`.
- **The page**: a day stacks its entries in occasion order (`buildCalendarView` sorts; `findEntries` reads by day and id) through **`OccasionRow`** (`occasion-row.tsx`: label, the Change menu (`EntryMenu`, #636: "Change outfit" while unworn, `data-change-entry`, and "Remove from calendar", an `hx-post` button guarded `data-needs-network`, **acting at once with Undo in the toast when that restores the entry exactly** (#686): the SERVER decides, from the current row (`removeEntry`'s `asksFirst` rolls back and answers `needs-confirmation`: worn, an agent's, or a one-off outfit it created), so a stale page never deletes unasked; that answer is a 409 with `closet:confirm` (`askFirst`, `announce.ts`) that opens the confirm sheet, whose yes posts again with `confirmed=1` (the row's own sheet, `removalAsksFirst` (`removal.ts`, the one rule the server's decision and the row's hint both call) the hint, sends it already; `request-failures.js` leaves a `closet:confirm` answer alone, so the sheet is all the person sees, #891). A reversible delete keeps a snapshot in the in-process `createUndoStore` (`restore.ts`, 30 s, a restart loses Undo) and the toast's Undo posts only its random token to `POST /calendar/restore` (web-only in `operations.ts`; the owner's, once; the server re-plans the entry as the planner's `auto` or the person's `user`, nothing from the client); a phone row has two direct actions, this and the worn pill), an `OutfitCollage` thumb linking to Styling (its pieces through `entryPieces`, `calendar-view.ts`, #358: an unworn entry from today on marks archived and away pieces, today's also the wash, which `entriesSql` counts only for `washOn`'s unworn entries; a worn or past entry marks nothing; the link's `aria-label` names the warnings, `outfitLabel`; the month's cells the same, as dots, plus one hidden dot for a blocking piece of any entry the cell does not draw, `hiddenBlocking`), the selfie, the worn pill (`WornControl size="pill"`, see Wears and washes); `returnTo` is `dayUrl`, the week scrolled to the day), the one way the week draws an entry. The agenda (`calendar-page.tsx`): tabs, weather, "Plan my week" (only once the closet can be dressed: `weekContext`'s `canDress`, the first-run gate, Today's First-run path), ‹ week › (with its year, `weekRangeLabel`), a strip of days linking to each `<section data-day>` (specs split on it: `test/integration/calendar-page.ts`): its weather chip, "Away: <place>" linking to each trip the day is one of (`AwayLink`, in the heading's grid row; such a day opens no slot; Trips, #684), its rows, **open slots** (`OpenSlotRow`: the week template's occasions it has no outfit for, from today on; computed at hour 0 so `/calendar` stays byte-stable all day), its looks, then "+ Plan" (empty) or "+ Another outfit" **only on a day without open slots** (one way in per day). **The month** (`month-page.tsx`, `GET /calendar/month`): a grid, each day its first entry's collage, "+N" and a worn dot, linking to `dayUrl`; the cell's collage drops accessories and bags (`bodyOf`, beside `SIDE` in `outfits/collage.tsx`). From lg the collage sits beside the date column (`MONTH_CELL`, #1070), never smaller: the `month` collage is the phone's, so a week of the month is one collage tall (August 2026's demo month: 1042 px to about 830 at 1440). **Its weather** is the week's mechanism, not a second one: `buildMonthView` gives the days the forecast reaches (`forecast`, today to `FORECAST_DAYS` on; null outside: nothing asked), each such cell reserves a `h-4` `WeatherCellSlot` under its date, and one hidden `WeatherMonthLoader` loads `/weather/summary?from=&to=&view=month` with `hx-swap="none"`: the same cached forecast, only the chips out of band, so nothing moves. The cell's `aria-label` hides its content, so it points `aria-describedby` at the chip. `weather.spec.ts` proves the month costs no Open-Meteo call beyond the week's.
- **The desktop week grid** (#467, 2026-11-13): from `lg` the agenda is a grid from the same markup (a tab root, one byte-identical page for every viewport; `layout-rail.spec.ts`, `calendar.spec.ts`), no `?view=`. Days are columns, `CalendarView.weekOccasions` (the template's occasions plus any an entry of the week uses, in `OCCASIONS` order) the rows, each `<section data-day>` a `grid-rows-subgrid` of the wrapper's `WEEK_ROWS[k]` (heading, k occasions, looks, "+ Plan"); its pieces carry `weekRow(n)` (`lg:row-start-n`) inside `data-cell` wrappers that are `contents` below lg, so **the phone's DOM order and boxes are unchanged** (entries, then open slots; desktop focus order follows it, not the grid). A week occasion a day has nothing for is a desktop-only cell (`WEEK_GRID.emptyCell`), from today on a "+ Occasion" opener of the day's sheet (`data-cell-plan`, not an open slot). `OccasionRow` and `OpenSlotRow` become cards at lg (`WEEK_CARD`, collage `cell`), `WeekStrip` is phone-only, `PageShell width="agenda"`. **The Calendar's pages carry no `lg:`** (#1070): every desktop class of the week, its cards and the month cell is a role in `src/web/layout/calendar-grid.ts` (`WEEK_GRID`, `WEEK_CARD`, `MONTH_CELL`, `weekRows`, `weekRow`), appended to the page's phone classes, so a page reads as the phone sees it; lg-check counts them in that file (its own `known` line), not as exempt layout. A week is legibility-bound: the `cell` collage's desktop step is never smaller than the phone's, so a five-occasion week stays about 1800 px at 1440 (the design's 900 was a goal under that rule, not met). **Never put `data-occasion` on a wrapper**: the worn swap retargets `closest [data-occasion]`.
- **The plan page** (`plan-page.tsx`, `PageShell width="wide"`, #1070): its head is a `PageBand` (`src/web/layout/page-band.tsx`: the day and the worn note span it from lg, the occasion chips and the two links share a row under them; below lg the body's own stack), the saved outfits `OUTFIT_ROW_GRID` (`SavedOutfitPicker`, shared with a trip's add page: rows on a phone, from lg four at 1440, three at 1280; 26 outfits 3172 px to 1074 at 1440): occasion chip links, "Choose from ideas" and "Style a new outfit" (same `?for=`), and the saved outfits a page at a time (#660, Outfits: one reader and sentinel) as buttons of one native `PostForm` to POST /calendar (`data-needs-network`), its read `planContext` (`day-choice.ts`: the page and the day's entries in one statement); an outfit already on the day, on any page, is disabled, saying for which occasion (`readDayChoice`, shared with the Saved tab; `entriesOfDaySql`). **The "+ Plan" sheet** (`plan-sheet.tsx`, a `<dialog>` per day, static so `/calendar` stays byte-stable): occasion radios, then ideas / saved outfits (`/outfits?for=`) / Styling, one GET form whose buttons' `formaction` all carry `?for=day:D&occasion=O`; a `Sheet` (`kind="choice"`, `#plan-sheet-<day>`, Layout); its openers carry `planSheetOpener` (the `sheetOpener` plus `data-plan`, the radio a one-line inline handler checks before confirm-sheet.js opens the sheet). With `&replace=` of an unworn entry it is "Change outfit" (no chips; every choice carries `replace`); for a worn entry it plans another and says why.
- **`OutfitDestination`** (`src/web/outfits/destination.tsx`, unit-tested) is the one parser of `?for=day:YYYY-MM-DD&occasion=O[&replace=E]` (`parseDestination`, lenient: malformed is `none`, an unknown occasion all day, a malformed `replace` dropped) and its writer (`destinationQuery`), and owns everything else that encodes it: the query schema (`DestinationQuery`), the posted body (`DestinationBody`), the hidden fields (`DestinationFields`) and, in `destination-views.tsx`, the pick label (`destinationPickLabel`). A feature spelling the `day:`/`trip:` format or a destination's schema itself is a regression (2026-10-17); the one form writing the field names by hand is the plan sheet (`plan-sheet.tsx`, a GET into Ideas whose occasion is the user's radio choice, its `for` from `destinationTarget`). `replace` (a day's only) names the entry a choice replaces; the writes check it (`replaceEntryOutfit`); an outfit opened in Styling to edit refuses it (it changes in place). Trips are `for=trip:ID[:YYYY-MM-DD][&occasion=O]` (no occasion is not all day there); `destinationTarget` writes the `for` value alone for a hidden field; Styling saves for a trip through `pickForTrip`, as the gallery's pick does.

- **Tabs**: `calendarTabs` (Week, Month, Trips), the app bar's `tabs` on each, static like the Outfits tabs (`/calendar` is a stale-while-revalidate tab root).
- **Selfies**: each row (`OccasionRow`) shows its entry's selfie, or up to today a way to take one (marks it worn); a day also shows looks whose outfit was deleted (`DayLooks`, `detachedLooksSql`). Deleting an entry goes through `removeEntry` (`entry-actions.ts`), which unlinks its selfie after commit; an entry takes the one-off outfit it created (origin `idea` or `planner`) once nothing holds it (Undo's rule, `removeUnheldOutfits`, #659, #748: 2 statements more, only then). "Created" is `outfit_calendar.outfit_created` (written true by the pick that made the outfit: `createOutfit`'s plan, `wearIdea` via `wearOutfitOn`, a Change; null for a reuse, a plan of a saved outfit and every entry before 0060; internal, not in the feed or API) (the planner's picks write it too, and every change of the entry's outfit rewrites it; entries planned before 0060 are null, so an old planner entry's outfit is kept until a re-plan); a reused or pre-existing outfit, and a `person` or `agent` one, never go. See Outfit selfies.
