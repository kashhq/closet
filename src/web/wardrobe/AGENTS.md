# Wardrobe

_Reference, not auto-loaded:_ [`reference.md`](reference.md) holds The garment page; Colours are a fixed set; Properties; Product link and price.

`src/web/wardrobe/` holds garments: the route plugins, `garment-access.ts`, `garment-actions.ts`, `garment-input.ts`, the pages, the JSON API's `api-routes.ts` / `garment-resource.ts` and `link-import/`; the file-by-file map is in `reference.md`.

- **Access**: every route takes `?ownerId=` for a shared wardrobe and resolves it once through `authorizeWardrobe` (`src/web/sharing/access.ts`): reads need a view, writes a MANAGE share or ownership, archive and delete ownership, a clone only a view (it lands in the requester's own wardrobe). A garment outside the addressed wardrobe is a 404 like a missing id.
- **Domain functions take an `Actor`** (`garment-access.ts`: `requireGarment`, `garmentIn`, `garmentGuard` bound once per plugin as `authorize(request, need)` / `authorize.garment(request, need)`), and throw `NotFound('garment')` / `Forbidden()`: 404 for what the actor cannot see, 403 for what they cannot change. A route re-declaring that wrapper or deciding a refusal itself is a regression (`guards.conf [refusals]`).
- **Every change to a garment goes through `garment-actions.ts`** (`(db or deps, actor, garmentAddress(request), input)`: it authorizes itself; the queries under it trust their `ownerId`). A new garment field joins `GarmentChanges` in `garment-changes.ts`; the form, MCP's `update_garment` and the API all save through `readGarmentForm`.
- **Which plugin owns a route** (all registered in `src/web/plugin.ts`; an unregistered one falls through to `/:id`): `grid-routes.tsx` has `GET /wardrobe`, `/wardrobe/tiles`, `POST /wardrobe/bulk` and `/wardrobe/bulk/{archive,washed,capsule}`, `GET /wardrobe/tag` and `POST /wardrobe/:id/tag`, and the grid's filter helpers (`gridSearch` to `gridContextParts`); `garment-routes.tsx` has `GET /wardrobe/new`, `POST /wardrobe`, `/wardrobe/properties-fragment`, `GET|POST|DELETE /wardrobe/:id`, `/edit`, `/clone`, `/condition`, `/share` and `/share/stop` (the public link, #669: owner and MANAGE, `setGarmentPublicShare`; see Sharing), `/archive` and `/restore`; `photo-routes.tsx` has `PHOTO_ADD_PATH` (`/wardrobe/new/photo`), `DRAFT_DISCARD_PATH`, `/wardrobe/:id/photo`, `/photo/rotate`, `/nobg`, `/cutout` and `/cutout/retry`. The rest are the plugins named in their own sections.
- **The grid** (`GET /wardrobe`, `/wardrobe/tiles`): `grid.md` in this directory, what each of its statements reads and why, paging, the filters, search and the care badges; read it before touching `gridPage`, `gridContext`, `GridFilters` or `wardrobe-page.tsx`.
- **Select mode, bulk edit and tagging mode**: `select-and-tag.md` in this directory; read it before touching `?select=1`, `/wardrobe/bulk`, `/wardrobe/tag` or `tag-page.tsx`.
- **The care label and the repair log** (#23): `care-and-repairs.md` in this directory; read it before touching the `care*` properties, `?wash=`, `repairs.ts`, `repair-log.tsx` or `/wardrobe/:id/repairs`.
- **Adding and editing, what each request reads** (#161): `form.md` in this directory, every form's one statement (`formContext`) and each save's statements; read it before adding a read to the garment form or a statement to a save.
- **The duplicate check, "add a copy"** (#20): `lookalikes.md` in this directory; read it before touching `lookalikes.ts`, `lookalike-region.tsx`, `/wardrobe/lookalikes` or `/wardrobe/:id/copies`.
- **Several photos at once, and the export** (#200): `drafts-and-export.md` in this directory; read it before touching the draft queue (`draft-queue.tsx`, the library's `multiple`, `/wardrobe/new/drafts/*`) or `/wardrobe/export.*`.
- **Order email import, "From your orders"** (#25): `order-mail.md` in this directory; read it before touching `order-mail/`, `/wardrobe/orders`, `orderItem` on the garment form or the `ORDER_MAIL_*` config.
- **The form is validated in two layers** (`garment-input.ts`): TypeBox caps shape and length (a 400 error page; the inputs carry the same `maxlength`), then `readGarmentForm` re-renders the form with a 400 and messages for a blank category, a colour outside `GARMENT_COLORS` or a date that is not one. Categories are stored trimmed and lower case (the filter value and the builder's key), one-line fields trimmed, blank fields NULL, sizes normalized (`XL` is `X-Large`). The date field keeps its historical name `dateAquired` (cached pages post it); the column is `acquired_on date`.
- **Link import**: `link-import/AGENTS.md`.
- **Photo writes** (`writes.ts`): the garment is looked up before a multipart body is read, so a refused upload stores nothing; `Photos.storeUploadParts` writes the bytes; `commitWithPhoto` inserts the `file` row with the garment write in one transaction and deletes the new bytes if it fails; replaced or deleted photos are unlinked after commit. An upload and a rotate replace the photo through one path, `swapPhoto` (a rotate adds a check under the garment lock that the photo it turned is still the garment's and that no mask the turned copy lacks landed since, `rotateStillValid`: a status check let a second mask saved meanwhile be deleted, a version check refused a double tap whose cutout finished between the taps, #248).

## Gotchas

- **A garment form without properties must not clear them.** An edit writes every field, and a form the installed app cached before properties existed posts none. Every form that renders them posts `props=1` (a hidden field in `PropertiesMain`); `readGarmentForm` reads and writes properties only with it (`garment-properties.spec.ts` saves an old-shaped form over a tagged garment). The product link and price have their own marker, `product=1` (a hidden field beside them in "More details"), because forms cached between the two post `props=1` without them (`garment-product.spec.ts`). The care fields (quantity, wash after, condition, #7) have `care=1`, the care label (#23) `careLabel=1`. A new garment field that old cached forms lack needs the same care: a marker of its own, posted beside the fields it guards.
- **A garment's status changes only through `setGarmentsStatus` (one garment: `setGarmentStatus`), and closet reads only through `inCloset`.** A new query that lists "the wardrobe" without the predicate shows wishlist items (things not owned) in the closet, the builder or laundry; one that writes `garment.status` directly skips the machine's refusals. `insertGarment` takes an entry status (closet or wishlist, never archived). The grid's `?archived=true` is the URL contract kept from before the status; internally it is `scope: 'owned'`.
