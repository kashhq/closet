# Layout

Navigation and rendering rules are in the root `AGENTS.md` (Web layer).

- **The app bar** (#82, `src/web/layout/app-bar.tsx`): `<AppBar ctx title titleMenu? back? formPage? scope? actions? tabs? />` is every page's header; there is no navbar, drawer or menu (the account's links live on Profile, `/auth/profile`, reached on a phone by the avatar, the email's initial, `lg:hidden`, and from lg by the rail's last link, the same `ProfileAvatar`; Login when signed out). `title` is the page's only h1 (`expectFullPage` in `test/integration/pages.ts` asserts one app bar, one h1 and the avatar or a way in, on the whole route table), `titleMenu` turns the title into a dropdown (the Wardrobe's switcher), `back` an optional back arrow (see The back arrow), `formPage` marks an add or edit page, `scope` what the page is scoped to (Styling's capsule menu), `actions` at most two, `tabs` the section's tabs (see Section tabs). It also carries `#loading` (the body's `hx-indicator`) and `#freshness` by id, which freshness.js and the layout find. It is rendered into the body, so a cached tab root carries its own bar: nothing in it may vary between two renders of the same page (Stale-while-revalidate tab roots). It is `--app-bar-h` tall (4rem, more below lg with tabs), which `PageShell` leaves above every page it frames.
- **A page's frame is `PageShell`** (#1059, `page-shell.tsx`; the design `docs/plans/2026-11-16-desktop-layout.md` §3.2): `<PageShell ctx name title? bar layout? width? aside? asideLabel? body={{ gutter?, gap? }} actionBar? overlays?>` renders `Layout`, the `AppBar` (`bar` is its props without `ctx`), `<main class="page-shell" data-layout>` and the `Dock`; a page brings its content and never writes that triple, a `<main>` or a bar offset. `title` (the document's) defaults to `bar.title` when that is a string. The shell owns the offsets: `--app-bar-h` above, the dock and a 2rem gutter below (and the action bar's room: A page's action bar), `px-4` and 1rem below the bar on the body (`gutter`: `page`, `bleed` for edge-to-edge strips, `flush` for a first row flush under the bar, the content padding itself). The body's flow is a named `gap` (a flex column, `data-gap` read by `main.css`'s `.page-body[data-gap]` as `--page-gap`), never a class: padding and width are the shell's. Inside `<main>` the regions sit in the desktop's visual order (a left `aside` before the body, a right one after), which is also the phone's stack; their places and the frame's width are `main.css`'s (`.page-shell[data-layout]`, `@layer components`, grids from lg only), never `lg:` classes on a page. `single` is the default and the only layout with a `width`; `split` (a `<section>` subject left), `panel` (an `<aside>` right), `facets` (an `<aside>` left: a `filters` sheet docks here, never in `overlays`; below lg it takes no room, its sheet closed; the Wardrobe's Closet tab, #1067) and `index` (a `<nav>` left, hidden below lg) are wide frames; the type makes each take an `aside`. **`split`** (#1068) is the garment page (the photo) and the outfit page (the collage, with what the outfit is above it): from lg the subject is 5fr left and sticky at `--app-bar-h`, the body 7fr at the narrow measure; on a phone the subject then the body, with the body's own `--page-gap` between them (`.page-shell[data-layout="split"] > .page-body`'s padding), so a page moved onto it keeps its phone pixel for pixel. **The phone order wins**: what a phone shows before the subject's picture goes in the subject region too, never a second DOM order or a page `lg:`. `panel` and `index` wait for their slices. `centred` is the standalone page (sign in, register, the invite landing, the offline page; `AccountShell` is its preset): the bar and the dock as on any page, the body one column centred in the room between them and from the top once taller (`justify-content: safe center`, `.page-shell[data-layout="centred"]` in `main.css`), no aside and no width; a page in it brings content and its own card `max-w-*`. `meta` (`PageMeta`: `ogTitle`, `ogDescription`, `ogUrl`, `ogImage`) goes straight to `Layout` for a page with its own link preview (the share page, About, the public entry pages). **Regions are the swap targets**: each gets its id from the shell, `<name>-body`, `<name>-aside` and `<name>-actions` (`regionId`), so a fragment swaps the region it owns (the Wardrobe's filtering swaps `#wardrobe-body`) or goes out of band; a body swap answers `PageFragment` (the same name and frame as the shell's, `WARDROBE_BODY`, and its `actionBar`), which sends the actions region out of band, so the bar follows the body (select mode filtered to no tiles drops it, back to some brings it, its `form=` naming the answer's form; `select-mode.spec.ts`); no page chooses a region id. A fragment's `aside` (`PageAsideFrame`) goes out of band too, as `innerHTML`: the region element stays (its scroll, the focus htmx restores by id), its content always the answer's state. Never keep an aside across a pushed answer: htmx's history snapshot keeps attributes, not a checked property, so Back showed the facets unchecked beside a filtered grid (#1067). `overlays` are the page's sheets, dialogs and toasts, after `<main>`, outside every region. `FormPage` and `SettingsPage` are presets of it (`single`, narrow). `expectPageShell` (`test/integration/pages.ts`, in `expectFullPage`) holds a framed page to one `.page-shell` and one `<main>`, its aside before the body unless the layout is `panel`; guards.conf `@page-main` refuses a `<main` outside `page-shell.tsx`, its `known` list is empty (D2, #1060).
- **A page's action bar is `PageShell`'s `actionBar`** (#1063, `action-bar.tsx`; the design's §3.4): the controls that act on the page's content, kept in reach while it scrolls (Laundry's Mark washed, select mode's and the capsule picker's count and verbs, Styling's preview, Shuffle and Save). The page passes `ActionBarProps` (`kind`, `align`, `children`), the shell renders `ActionBar` as the `#<name>-actions` region, a child of `<main>` after the body (every page has the region: empty and `hidden` without a bar, so a fragment can bring or drop one); a page never renders one or fixes its own controls above the dock (`expectActionBar`, `test/integration/pages.ts`, in `expectFullPage`: at most one `.action-bar`, no control inside a `bottom-dock` element). `single` only for now; `panel`'s place (the top of its aside) comes with Styling's move (D15). Its box, places and room are `main.css`'s (the action bar block), never classes. `kind` is #664's rule: a `row` (one row of `btn-sm` controls and short text) is fixed above the dock below lg and from lg sticks under the app bar at the head of the content column, above what it acts on. Every bar follows the body in the DOM, the phone's visual order, so Tab reaches the rows first; from lg `order` in a flex `<main>` lifts a row to the head, never the markup (`test/wears.spec.ts` tabs it on the phone). A `stack` (varying height: Styling's) follows the body, sticky above the dock in its flow below lg (it lands below the body's end, so it reserves nothing and the page leaves the dock alone below it), and from lg is the 20rem column beside the body. **A row is `--action-bar-h` tall at every text size** (its padding, 1px border and a btn-sm's `--size-field * 8`, in rem): it never wraps, scrolling sideways when large text overflows it, so the scroll padding (`scroll-padding-bottom` below lg, `-top` from lg) and the shell's reservation below lg, which read the same token, are its real height (#1030; the 3.5rem it replaced was short of it on a coarse pointer and once it wrapped). Controls outside a form join it through `form=` (Laundry's `#laundry-form`, `#pick-form`); a form's controls are `form.elements`, never a `querySelectorAll` inside it (connectivity.js, outbox.ts, submit-once.js). `test/add-from-photo.spec.ts` focuses a checkbox at 200% text clear of select mode's bar; `test/wears.spec.ts` holds Mark washed above the hamper's first row, its end on theirs, at 1440.
- **Section tabs are the app bar's** (#1061, the design's §3.1): a section's tabs (Wardrobe: Closet, Capsules, Laundry, Wishlist through `wardrobeBar`; Calendar: Week, Month, Trips, `calendarTabs`; Outfits: Saved, Ideas, Inspiration, `outfitTabs`) are one builder per section returning `LinkTabsProps`, passed as the bar's `tabs` by every sibling page, so the row cannot move between them (it jumped with each page's width when pages drew it). Below lg it is the header's second row (`LinkTabs placement="bar"`, `.app-bar-tabs`): `:root:has(.app-bar-tabs)` grows `--app-bar-h` by `--app-bar-tabs-h` (daisyUI's tab height, owned in `main.css`), which every offset reads (the shell, the scroll padding, `top-bar`, `pt-under-bar`), the active underline on the bar's border, one line always (a row too long scrolls inside the bar; a focused tab's ring is inset, the scroller clips an outer one). The bar's height is fixed in rem, so its title block must fit 4rem at any text size: two title lines, or one while `#freshness` shows (`.app-bar-title:has(> #freshness:not(.hidden))` clamps the h1 to one line); without that the freshness line pushed the tab row out of the bar at 200% text. From lg it sits inline after the title on the one 4rem row, the actions pushed to the end; DOM order is title, tabs, actions at both sizes. A page's own sub-tabs (Saved's two lists) stay `LinkTabs` in its body. `expectAppBar` (`test/integration/pages.ts`) fails a section's tab nav or an `.app-bar-tabs` outside the header; `test/trips.spec.ts` compares the row's place across the Calendar's and the Wardrobe's pages at 1440. A row that sticks under the bar uses `top-bar`, never `top-16`.
- **The back arrow goes back like a native app's** (#105, `public/js/back.js`, loaded by the layout on every page): the app bar's `BackLink historyBack` (`data-history-back`) goes back in history, to the page as htmx's snapshot kept it (Styling's rows as left), whenever the entry before is in the app, and follows `back` (its href, the page's fixed parent) only on a cold entry: a shared link, a push notification, a fresh launch, a new tab. A destination line's own `BackLink` (Styling's, Ideas', `DayDestinationLine`) stays a fixed link. **"In the app" is recorded, never guessed**: every entry carries its trail (the app's entries from the cold one to itself) in `history.state.closetTrail`, per entry, so back/forward, a reload and the bfcache find theirs; a same-origin referrer cannot tell after a boosted navigation, sessionStorage cannot say which entry back/forward landed on, and the Navigation API reached Safari only in 26.x. A native post (every `PostForm`) starts a new document without state: its submit hands the trail over in sessionStorage, taken only by the next document whose referrer is the posting page. **Back skips a form that is done**: a `formPage` (add and edit pages, Styling where it can save, the self-saving style, sizes and tokens pages) is done once saved or cancelled. Its Cancel is `CancelLink` (`parts.tsx`), a back arrow in words; on a cold entry the arrow or Cancel goes forward to its href and that entry counts as the form's exit (other links a form page holds, Styling's garments, do not). From the page a save or exit lands on, the arrow goes past the form (and a refused attempt's re-render) and, inside that chain only, past entries of the same pathname (the page before the save, a `?saved=1` copy): Garment, Edit, Save, back lands where the garment was opened from, while Insights, `?unworn=30`, back is Insights. A new add or edit page is a `FormPage` (which passes `formPage` and renders `CancelLink`) and is listed with `form(...)` in `pageRoutes` (`test/integration/pages.ts`): `expectFormPageFlag` fails a page whose flag disagrees with the table. **A save refreshes what history restores**: each trail entry keeps when its page was rendered (`at`), a native post records the tab's last save (sessionStorage), and an entry restored from history (htmx's restore, a bfcache `pageshow`, a document a back/forward loads again from the HTTP cache, reloaded once loaded) that was rendered before the save is reloaded in place, which also revalidates a stale tab root. Each stale entry refreshes once however far back; entries rendered after the last save (anything after signing in) restore as they were. htmx writes (autosave, hx-post) are not saves here. **The console never names a path**: `back.js` logs a route's first segment only (`routeKind`), since paths carry secrets (an invite token). `test/back-navigation.spec.ts (retired 2026-10-13)` covers each case at 390 px (the rename showing after Wardrobe, Edit, Save, back); `app-shell.spec.ts` the markup it reads.
- **Dates a user reads come from `src/web/date-labels.ts`, never an ISO string** (#356; `test/integration/date-labels.spec.ts` strips tags and fails a page whose text has `YYYY-MM-DD`). A past day relative to today is `relativeDay` (today, yesterday, 2 to 6 days ago, then `dateLabel`: "Tue, Sep 29" in today's year, else "Sep 29, 2025"); a date on its own `dateLabel`; a planned day `dayLabel` ("Tuesday, Sep 29") or `shortDayLabel` ("Sep 29"). Today is the caller's `todayIn(APP_TIMEZONE, now)`. A list of days heads each with `DayHeading` (`day-heading.tsx`: weekday, the date muted, a Today badge), as the calendar week and a trip do.
- **A garment's status mark is `<GarmentMark>`** (#357, `garment-mark.tsx`, beside `parts.tsx`): every badge that says where a garment stands (to buy, archived, lent, at repair, needs a wash, set aside) derives its marks with `garmentMarks(garment, { ownerView })` (`src/wardrobe/marks.ts`, pure: the order, and the gate that keeps away and the wash from a viewer of a shared wardrobe) and draws them here, never a hand-made `badge` with a status string. One variant and one `mark.*` string per mark, in `MARKS`: to-buy `badge-accent`, archived `badge-neutral`, away (both) `badge-warning` (can't wear it now), set-aside `badge-outline` (its dot a border), needs-wash `badge-info`; always `badge-xs`. Needs-wash's words take the copies of a multiple (`wash`: "Wash 2/3", `mark.NEEDS_WASH_COPIES`). The first mark is in words, the rest only in its accessible name, so a tile never stacks status badges. `dot` is the frame too small for words (the collage's thumbs), `text` other words in the first mark's look (Muse's price), `class` placement only (position, a responsive size step). A surface marks only what it already reads (an absent field is no mark): the grid status, away and the wash (the owner's grid alone reads them); Styling and the garment page status (the page's away and wash are its wear section's); Muse status and set aside; the share page to-buy only; the collage to-buy, plus archived, away and (today's cards) the wash where it warns (`collagePieces`, #358: Today, the calendar from today on, the outfit page). Tests find a mark by `data-mark="<kind>"`; `test/integration/garment-marks.spec.ts` holds "Archived" and "To buy" to one markup across the grid, Styling and the garment page. Condition ("Needs repair") and copies ("×3") are not status marks and keep their own badges.

- **An add or edit page that is one native post is `FormPage`** (#411, `form-page.tsx`; the garment, capsule and trip forms, "Bought it"): a `PageShell` preset (narrow, `single`): the `formPage` app bar, one `PostForm` (offline-guarded like every `PostForm`), Cancel beside the submit, and the edit page's guarded Delete (`remove`). The page brings only its fields, an `intro` above and its other forms `after`. Offline guarding was each page's opt-in and only trips had it, so capsule, garment and "Bought it" posts landed on the worker's offline page. `@form-page` (guards.conf) keeps `<CancelLink` there; `expectOfflineGuarded` (`test/integration/pages.ts`) is each form's test. Not frames: Styling, the self-saving style and sizes pages, tokens, link import and the trip add page.
- **A setting's own page is `SettingsPage`** (#728, `settings-page.tsx`): Profile is an index of rows (`src/web/auth/pages.tsx`), and each setting is a page under it (sharing, weather, the week, notifications, devices; style and sizes predate it): the title its h1, `back` to its Profile row (`profileSection(id)`), `formPage` when its native posts land back on it; a `PageShell` preset, narrow. A feature renders its settings there, never a section on Profile.
- **A failed htmx request says so** (#642, `public/js/request-failures.js`, loaded on every page): a boosted tap answered with our error page swaps it in; a fragment request's failure is the server's `HX-Trigger` toast, never a swap; a timeout (`HTMX_CONFIG.timeout`, the outbox's `REPLAY_TIMEOUT_MS`) toasts too. The server half and the rule (throw, never 200-with-error): `docs/web-layer.md#a-failed-htmx-request-says-so-642`.
- **A write keeps focus and says what it did** (#679): `#toast-host` (`AppStatus`) is the page's one polite status region (`role="status"`, `hx-preserve`: the same node across boosted navigations, so a landing message is added to a region already there, a frame after settle), and every toast lands there through `public/js/toast.js` (loaded on every page), none with a role of its own: a live region inserted with its message is not reliably read. An htmx write says its result with `announce(reply, message)` (`src/web/announce.ts`, `HX-Trigger` `closet:status`); a native post's landing page with `SavedToast` (now an inert `<template data-status-message>` toast.js moves into the region). An in-place write's submit button keeps one `id` in every state, so htmx refocuses its replacement; where the answer drops it, the answer marks where focus goes with `autofocus` (Washed to the wear line), as a landing page does (Mark washed, Today after Wear this, `?wore=`). `expectInPlaceWritesKeepFocus`, `expectOneStatusRegion` and `expectStatusMessage` (`test/integration/pages.ts`) hold it; `test/wears.spec.ts` taps each from the keyboard.
- **The error page is a sentence and the way on** (#644, `src/web/error-page.tsx`, chosen by `createErrorHandler`): `PageShell` narrow with `EmptyState`'s look, the app bar's one h1 a human title by class (missing 404/410, refused 403, retry 409/412/429/503, rejected other 4xx, failed other 5xx), then Back (`data-history-back`, the section's home as its cold-entry href) and the section's home link (`Open Wardrobe`; Today's for no section). **Try again** is a plain link to the request's own GET URL, only on the retry class and never for a write, a `secretPath` route or a URL `scrubSecrets` would change (`retryHref`). The request id, one muted selectable line, is on a 5xx only. No path, no time.
- **The dock decides by session, once** (#662, `dock.tsx`): `Dock` renders nothing without `ctx.user`, so no signed-out page (sign in, register, the invite landing, the signed-out 404) carries five tabs that each bounce to the login page, and `:root:has(.dock)` drops the rail padding with it. Those pages show `BrandMark` (`brand-mark.tsx`: the app icon and name) above their content; their h1 is the page's own title (`LOGIN_TITLE`), the og title stays meta only (`AccountShell`'s `preview`). The sign-in forms' frame is `SignedOutFrame` (a `card`) around a `bare` `Fieldset`, not the fieldset's own border: a browser draws a legend straddling that border. The signed-in forms keep the bordered `Fieldset`. `SubmitButton` is `btn-primary` (`variant="error"` the only other).
- **Every sheet is `Sheet`** (#1062, `sheet.tsx`; the design `docs/plans/2026-11-16-desktop-layout.md` §3.3): `<Sheet id kind labelledBy gap? dismiss>` renders the `<dialog data-sheet={kind}>`, its box and `SheetBackdrop`; the page brings the content (its heading, whose id is `labelledBy`, and its `modal-action` row, with `SheetCancel` for a Cancel that only closes). Below lg every kind is the bottom sheet; from lg `choice` is centred (a question: the confirmation, the Plan sheets, Styling's Save, the bulk verbs), `form` a full-height right panel `max-w-md` (fields: the photo sheet, bulk "Set…"), `filters` is never opened from lg but docked, closed, in its `facets` aside (the Wardrobe's filters, #1067: `main.css` shows the closed dialog in place, hides its backdrop's Close, keeps Clear and Apply at its foot, the aside its scroller; its opener spreads `dockedFiltersOpener`, gone where it docks, never a page `lg:`; its form is one element at both sizes, so nothing renders twice; `expectDockedFilters` in `test/integration/pages.ts` fails one rendered `open` or outside a facets aside), `dialog` is centred at every size, the phone too (what is shown, not asked: the mask editor's canvas, a selfie; its box takes `boxClass` for its own width and padding), `menu` hangs under its trigger, capped to the room beside it so a tall menu scrolls inside itself (the add sheet; CSS anchor positioning in `main.css` under `@supports`, `position: fixed` because an anchor outside the dialog is only valid for a box whose containing block is the viewport; centred without it). `gap` names the box's flow (a column 3 or 4 apart, else blocks); a stacked choice or form keeps 2rem below its last row, as the sheets had before, so the phone did not move. **A sheet is opened by a control spreading `sheetOpener(id)`** (`data-sheet-open`, `aria-haspopup`), never an inline `showModal()`: `public/js/confirm-sheet.js` (the module that drives the confirmation, now every sheet's) listens for the click and `openSheet(dialog, trigger)` marks a menu's trigger as its anchor, cycles Tab and Shift+Tab inside the sheet (a modal dialog lets Tab out of its last control into the browser's toolbar; a radio group is one stop) and returns focus to the trigger on close (or to its `<details>` menu's summary when the menu closed; Safari focuses no tapped button, so the browser's own return had nowhere to go). Escape, the first focus (`autofocus`, else the first control) and the inert page are `showModal()`'s; daisyUI's `modal[open]` locks the page's scroll. An inline `onclick` beside the opener runs first (the calendar's `planSheetOpener` checks the occasion). `@sheet` (guards.conf, its own section) refuses daisyUI's modal box class and the placement classes outside `sheet.tsx`; its `known` list is empty, and the photo viewer is a full-screen dialog of its own. Five kinds: `choice`, `form`, `menu`, `dialog`, `filters`. A script that opens a sheet imports `openSheet` (import map `confirm-sheet`), never `showModal()` (the photo sheet's reopen after a rotate, the mask editor). `test/add-from-photo.spec.ts` tabs through the open filter sheet and closes it with Escape at 390, and at 1440 filters from the docked facets with focus kept on Apply, Back showing the facets of the URL it lands on, and `/` focusing search; `make screenshots` shoots every sheet at 390 and 1440 (`90-sheet-*`).
- **View tabs and sheet closes have one writer each** (2026-10-18): a bar of link tabs is `LinkTabs` (`link-tabs.tsx`: a `<nav aria-label>` of links with `aria-current="page"`, never `role=tab`; one underline look and no class from a caller; its spacing is its `placement`, `page` or the app bar's `bar`; the inactive colour is main.css's muted rule, #726; a `false` entry is a tab left out), a dialog's tap-outside close `SheetBackdrop` (`parts.tsx`, labelled Close on a sheet that shows, Cancel on one that asks a choice). `@link-tabs` and `@sheet-backdrop` (guards.conf) fail a hand-written copy.
- **Confirmations are the app's own sheet, never the browser's** (#686): `<ConfirmSheet>` (`confirm-sheet.tsx`, in every body) is driven by `public/js/confirm-sheet.js`, which answers `htmx:confirm` (`hx-confirm`) and the `submit` of a `PostForm` with `confirm` (`data-confirm`; a native post, held and resent with `requestSubmit` once confirmed), and exports `confirmAction()`, which opens it through `openSheet` like every sheet (the outbox's sign-out discard imports it as `confirm-sheet`, an `--external` of `generate:outbox`). The destructive button is `btn-error` and wears the trigger's own words; Cancel is `autofocus`, Escape and the backdrop cancel, focus returns to the trigger. The module loads before `submit-once.js`, which skips a prevented submit. `@native-confirm` (guards.conf) refuses `confirm(` in `src/web`, `public/js` and `views/assets`; `pageErrors` (`test/support`) fails any scenario that sees a native dialog. **Reversible removals do not ask**: they act and `announce(reply, message, undo)` (`StatusUndo`: label, action, fields) puts an Undo button in the toast (`toast.js` posts the fields through `htmx.ajax`, 10 s); a calendar entry is the one so far (Calendar's Remove).

## Gotchas

- **htmx overwrites `history.state` with `{htmx: true}`** whenever it snapshots a page (before every boosted navigation, on every history restore), so anything kept on an entry must be written back after it: `back.js` keeps the trail on screen in a variable and rewrites it on `htmx:beforeHistoryUpdate` (the entry left), `htmx:pushedIntoHistory`/`replacedInHistory` (the new one) and `htmx:historyRestore`; a `popstate` listener reads `event.state` before htmx's handler clobbers it (htmx sets `window.onpopstate` at DOMContentLoaded, after the module registered). The arrow's click listener is on the document in the capture phase, ahead of htmx's listener on the boosted link.
- **The dock stacks above every page, and its active tab comes from one route table.** `.dock` and `.app-bar` are z-index 900 in `main.css` (daisyUI gives the dock 1, so the outfit card's `relative z-10` calendar button drew over it); page content stays at z-20 or below, the toasts at 999 above both. The bar and the dock never overlap, so they need no order between them. A bar above the dock is the page's `actionBar` (above), which sits on `--spacing-dock` (`main.css`: the dock's height with the safe area): fixed for a `row`, sticky in the page's flow for a `stack` whose height varies, so it never covers the page's end (Styling's, #664). A tab is lit on every page of its section: `sectionOf` (`src/web/layout/sections.ts`) maps the request path, whole segments (`/capsules/*` and `/laundry` are Wardrobe, `/wardrobe-share` is nothing). A new top-level route shows no tab until it is added there. `DOCK_TABS` is which sections the dock shows, in order; `SECTION_HOME` (the tabs' links) minus Today is the service worker's stale-while-revalidate tab roots (`OPENS_STALE`, `page-cache.ts`): a new tab root must render byte for byte the same while nothing changes, or be left out of it on purpose like Today. `test/dock.spec.ts (retired 2026-10-13)` probes the dock at 390 px on each page with floating controls.
- **A page's `<main>` needs `w-full` beside `max-w-* mx-auto`.** The body is `flex flex-col`, and a flex item with auto margins does not stretch: it sizes to its content, capped by the max width. A horizontal scroll strip (its max-content is every card) then widened the garment page to 512 px on a 390 px phone (#18b); short content made pages narrower than the screen. `PageShell`'s widths carry it (`WIDTH_CLASS`), as must every raw `<main>` left; the gallery's `sm:mx-auto` only applies past the phone.
- **At `lg` (1024 px) the dock is a left rail, from the same markup.** `main.css` restyles `.dock` (a 5rem column, the active mark on its left edge, links unchanged, so keyboard order is the dock's and `aria-current` marks the tab). Never a second markup or a width-dependent render: the server cannot know the viewport and the tab roots are cached byte for byte. The rail carries every place: Profile is its last link (`hidden lg:flex mt-auto`, at the foot), and the app bar's avatar hides from lg (owner decision 1 of the desktop plan). Where the page has a dock, `:root:has(.dock)` sets `--rail-width` (5rem; 0 otherwise) and `--spacing-dock` to 0: the body pads left by the rail, `.app-bar` and the pull-to-refresh bar start at it, and anything fixed to the screen's left uses `left-rail` (the connectivity banner; the action bar's row reads `--rail-width` in `main.css`; the toast host centres on the content column). A new fixed element uses `left-rail`, never `left-0`. Dialogs (z 999) cover the rail; the rail is z 900 like the app bar.
- **Page widths come from `PageShell`'s `width`, never a `max-w-*` on a page's `<main>`** (`page-shell.tsx`; tokens `--container-page-narrow` 40rem, `--container-page-wide` 80rem in `main.css`). `narrow` is the default (forms, reading, single-column lists); `wide` is for galleries; `agenda` is `narrow` below lg and `wide` from it, for a list that becomes a grid on a desktop (the Calendar week, #467). Only the page-level wrapper chooses; dialogs, cards and controls keep their own `max-w-*`. Chosen so far: Calendar week `agenda`; the capsule page `wide` (its members are `GALLERY_GRID`, which must never have fewer columns at `lg` than `sm`'s four); the garment form `narrow` (the garment page is `split`); Today, Outfits (the saved grid is `OUTFIT_GRID`), Ideas, Styling, the Calendar month, Insights, the Wardrobe (`#wardrobe-body`), the plan page and the plans list `wide`; the plan change page and item form `narrow`. Column steps are named once in `columns.ts` (`GALLERY_GRID`, `CARD_COLUMNS`, `OUTFIT_GRID`, `PAIR_GRID`), never copied `grid-cols-*`. The `FormPage` frames are `narrow` (the capsule, trip and "Bought it" forms widened from 32 to 40rem with it). Every page is on the shell since D2 (#1060): Profile, style, sizes, tokens, link import, tag, order review, recap, a trip and its add page, the plan page, the trips list, Capsules, Laundry, the share page and About are `narrow` until their grids arrive (D12, D13; the plan page and the trips list are §4's `wide` then).
- **Every grid's columns are named once in `columns.ts`; width past `lg` is density** (#1064, `docs/plans/2026-11-16-desktop-layout.md` §3.6). ESLint refuses a `grid-cols-*` or `columns-*` class anywhere in `src/` but `columns.ts`, so a page picks a step by name (`GALLERY_GRID`, `OUTFIT_GRID`, `FIELD_GRID`, `WEEK_COLUMNS`, ...) or adds one there. The galleries (`GALLERY_GRID`, `OUTFIT_GRID`, `CANDIDATE_GRID`, `IDEA_GRID`, `CARD_GRID`) keep fixed counts below `lg` (`IDEA_GRID` has none: below `lg` idea cards are a `SnapStrip`, whose `desktop="grid"` applies it, or their parent's stack) and from it auto-fill `repeat(auto-fill, minmax(var(--tile-*), 1fr))`: the `--tile-*` minimums sit in `main.css` beside the page widths (plain `:root` properties, read only by those arbitrary values), so 1024, 1440 and 1920 each get as many columns as fit and there is no `xl` layout (owner decision 6). A collage's desktop step is its size's, in `collage.tsx` (`cell`: the week card about 150 px tall; `month`: none, the phone's size; a desktop step never draws a garment smaller than the phone does, so legibility wins over fitting the month on one screen), never a page class. `IDEA_GRID` holds Today's and Ideas' cards (D7, #1065: Snap strip's `desktop`); `CARD_GRID` waits for its pages (D12, D13).
- **Fixed chrome never covers focus, and gives way to large text** (#730, `main.css`): `html` carries `scroll-padding-top` (the app bar, `--app-bar-h`, in rem so it and the two-line title clamp grow with the text, #1028) and, where a dock exists, `scroll-padding-bottom` (`--spacing-dock`, zero at the rail; the action bar adds `--action-bar-h`, #1026, #1030: see A page's action bar); below lg the dock is a size container and its labels become screen-reader-only under 22rem of container width, which in rem shrinks as the root font grows (a media query would not see a 200% root). The bar's h1 clamps to two lines instead of truncating; auth frames are `w-full max-w-xs`, never `w-xs`. `public/js/menu-escape.js` closes an open `.app-bar details` on Escape (a `<details>` has no key of its own). `test/trips.spec.ts` and `test/auth.spec.ts` assert each at 390 px.
- **A collage inside a link that already names the outfit is `decorative`** (#730: its photos `alt=""`; the calendar's rows and month cells, the week plan's and the garment page's thumbs). The Wardrobe's tab row is a `LinkTabs` `<nav aria-label>`, a landmark.
- **Page-level `lg:` classes only shrink** (#1066, `make lg-check` in `ci-quick`, `lg-check-staged` in pre-commit reading the index; `scripts/ci/lg_check.py`, `scripts/ci/lg_count.conf`): the desktop layout lives in `layout/`, `columns.ts`, `snap-strip.tsx` and `collage.tsx` (exempt), not in per-page patches. Every other `src/web` file has a `known` count of its `lg:` uses; growing one, or an `lg:` in a file with no line, fails, and removing one fails too until the same PR lowers the number (a file reaching zero loses its line). A page needing desktop behaviour adds it to a layout piece instead.
