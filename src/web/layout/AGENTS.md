# Layout

Navigation and rendering rules are in the root `AGENTS.md` (Web layer).

- **The app bar** (#82, `src/web/layout/app-bar.tsx`): `<AppBar ctx title titleMenu? back? formPage? scope? actions? />` is every page's header; there is no navbar, drawer or menu (the account's links live on Profile, `/auth/profile`, reached by the avatar, the email's initial; Login when signed out). `title` is the page's only h1 (`expectFullPage` in `test/integration/pages.ts` asserts one app bar, one h1 and the avatar or a way in, on the whole route table), `titleMenu` turns the title into a dropdown (the Wardrobe's switcher), `back` an optional back arrow (see The back arrow), `formPage` marks an add or edit page, `scope` what the page is scoped to (Styling's capsule menu), `actions` at most two. It also carries `#loading` (the body's `hx-indicator`) and `#freshness` by id, which freshness.js and the layout find. It is rendered into the body, so a cached tab root carries its own bar: nothing in it may vary between two renders of the same page (Stale-while-revalidate tab roots). It is 4rem: pages leave `pt-20` (the bar and a 1rem gutter), or `pt-16` when their first row sits flush under it (the Wardrobe tabs).
- **The back arrow goes back like a native app's** (#105, `public/js/back.js`, loaded by the layout on every page): the app bar's `BackLink historyBack` (`data-history-back`) goes back in history, to the page as htmx's snapshot kept it (Styling's rows as left), whenever the entry before is in the app, and follows `back` (its href, the page's fixed parent) only on a cold entry: a shared link, a push notification, a fresh launch, a new tab. A destination line's own `BackLink` (Styling's, Ideas', `DayDestinationLine`) stays a fixed link. **"In the app" is recorded, never guessed**: every entry carries its trail (the app's entries from the cold one to itself) in `history.state.closetTrail`, per entry, so back/forward, a reload and the bfcache find theirs; a same-origin referrer cannot tell after a boosted navigation, sessionStorage cannot say which entry back/forward landed on, and the Navigation API reached Safari only in 26.x. A native post (every `PostForm`) starts a new document without state: its submit hands the trail over in sessionStorage, taken only by the next document whose referrer is the posting page. **Back skips a form that is done**: a `formPage` (add and edit pages, Styling where it can save, the self-saving style, sizes and tokens pages) is done once saved or cancelled. Its Cancel is `CancelLink` (`parts.tsx`), a back arrow in words; on a cold entry the arrow or Cancel goes forward to its href and that entry counts as the form's exit (other links a form page holds, Styling's garments, do not). From the page a save or exit lands on, the arrow goes past the form (and a refused attempt's re-render) and, inside that chain only, past entries of the same pathname (the page before the save, a `?saved=1` copy): Garment, Edit, Save, back lands where the garment was opened from, while Insights, `?unworn=30`, back is Insights. A new add or edit page is a `FormPage` (which passes `formPage` and renders `CancelLink`) and is listed with `form(...)` in `pageRoutes` (`test/integration/pages.ts`): `expectFormPageFlag` fails a page whose flag disagrees with the table. **A save refreshes what history restores**: each trail entry keeps when its page was rendered (`at`), a native post records the tab's last save (sessionStorage), and an entry restored from history (htmx's restore, a bfcache `pageshow`, a document a back/forward loads again from the HTTP cache, reloaded once loaded) that was rendered before the save is reloaded in place, which also revalidates a stale tab root. Each stale entry refreshes once however far back; entries rendered after the last save (anything after signing in) restore as they were. htmx writes (autosave, hx-post) are not saves here. **The console never names a path**: `back.js` logs a route's first segment only (`routeKind`), since paths carry secrets (an invite token). `test/back-navigation.spec.ts (retired 2026-10-13)` covers each case at 390 px (the rename showing after Wardrobe, Edit, Save, back); `app-shell.spec.ts` the markup it reads.
- **Dates a user reads come from `src/web/date-labels.ts`, never an ISO string** (#356; `test/integration/date-labels.spec.ts` strips tags and fails a page whose text has `YYYY-MM-DD`). A past day relative to today is `relativeDay` (today, yesterday, 2 to 6 days ago, then `dateLabel`: "Tue, Sep 29" in today's year, else "Sep 29, 2025"); a date on its own `dateLabel`; a planned day `dayLabel` ("Tuesday, Sep 29") or `shortDayLabel` ("Sep 29"). Today is the caller's `todayIn(APP_TIMEZONE, now)`. A list of days heads each with `DayHeading` (`day-heading.tsx`: weekday, the date muted, a Today badge), as the calendar week and a trip do.
- **A garment's status mark is `<GarmentMark>`** (#357, `garment-mark.tsx`, beside `parts.tsx`): every badge that says where a garment stands (to buy, archived, lent, at repair, needs a wash, set aside) derives its marks with `garmentMarks(garment, { ownerView })` (`src/wardrobe/marks.ts`, pure: the order, and the gate that keeps away and the wash from a viewer of a shared wardrobe) and draws them here, never a hand-made `badge` with a status string. One variant and one `mark.*` string per mark, in `MARKS`: to-buy `badge-accent`, archived `badge-neutral`, away (both) `badge-warning` (can't wear it now), set-aside `badge-outline` (its dot a border), needs-wash `badge-info`; always `badge-xs`. Needs-wash's words take the copies of a multiple (`wash`: "Wash 2/3", `mark.NEEDS_WASH_COPIES`). The first mark is in words, the rest only in its accessible name, so a tile never stacks status badges. `dot` is the frame too small for words (the collage's thumbs), `text` other words in the first mark's look (Muse's price), `class` placement only (position, a responsive size step). A surface marks only what it already reads (an absent field is no mark): the grid status, away and the wash (the owner's grid alone reads them); Styling and the garment page status (the page's away and wash are its wear section's); Muse status and set aside; the share page to-buy only; the collage to-buy, plus archived, away and (today's cards) the wash where it warns (`collagePieces`, #358: Today, the calendar from today on, the outfit page). Tests find a mark by `data-mark="<kind>"`; `test/integration/garment-marks.spec.ts` holds "Archived" and "To buy" to one markup across the grid, Styling and the garment page. Condition ("Needs repair") and copies ("×3") are not status marks and keep their own badges.

- **An add or edit page that is one native post is `FormPage`** (#411, `form-page.tsx`; the garment, capsule and trip forms, "Bought it"): the `formPage` app bar, `PageMain`, one `PostForm` (offline-guarded like every `PostForm`), Cancel beside the submit, and the edit page's guarded Delete (`remove`). The page brings only its fields, an `intro` above and its other forms `after`. Offline guarding was each page's opt-in and only trips had it, so capsule, garment and "Bought it" posts landed on the worker's offline page. `@form-page` (guards.conf) keeps `<CancelLink` there; `expectOfflineGuarded` (`test/integration/pages.ts`) is each form's test. Not frames: Styling, the self-saving style and sizes pages, tokens, link import and the trip add page.
- **A setting's own page is `SettingsPage`** (#728, `settings-page.tsx`): Profile is an index of rows (`src/web/auth/pages.tsx`), and each setting is a page under it (sharing, weather, the week, notifications, devices; style and sizes predate it): the title its h1, `back` to its Profile row (`profileSection(id)`), `formPage` when its native posts land back on it, `PageMain` narrow. A feature renders its settings there, never a section on Profile.
- **A failed htmx request says so** (#642, `public/js/request-failures.js`, loaded on every page): a boosted tap answered with our error page swaps it in; a fragment request's failure is the server's `HX-Trigger` toast, never a swap; a timeout (`HTMX_CONFIG.timeout`, the outbox's `REPLAY_TIMEOUT_MS`) toasts too. The server half and the rule (throw, never 200-with-error): `docs/web-layer.md#a-failed-htmx-request-says-so-642`.
- **A write keeps focus and says what it did** (#679): `#toast-host` (`AppStatus`) is the page's one polite status region (`role="status"`, `hx-preserve`: the same node across boosted navigations, so a landing message is added to a region already there, a frame after settle), and every toast lands there through `public/js/toast.js` (loaded on every page), none with a role of its own: a live region inserted with its message is not reliably read. An htmx write says its result with `announce(reply, message)` (`src/web/announce.ts`, `HX-Trigger` `closet:status`); a native post's landing page with `SavedToast` (now an inert `<template data-status-message>` toast.js moves into the region). An in-place write's submit button keeps one `id` in every state, so htmx refocuses its replacement; where the answer drops it, the answer marks where focus goes with `autofocus` (Washed to the wear line), as a landing page does (Mark washed, Today after Wear this, `?wore=`). `expectInPlaceWritesKeepFocus`, `expectOneStatusRegion` and `expectStatusMessage` (`test/integration/pages.ts`) hold it; `test/wears.spec.ts` taps each from the keyboard.
- **The error page is a sentence and the way on** (#644, `src/web/error-page.tsx`, chosen by `createErrorHandler`): `PageMain` narrow with `EmptyState`'s look, the app bar's one h1 a human title by class (missing 404/410, refused 403, retry 409/412/429/503, rejected other 4xx, failed other 5xx), then Back (`data-history-back`, the section's home as its cold-entry href) and the section's home link (`Open Wardrobe`; Today's for no section). **Try again** is a plain link to the request's own GET URL, only on the retry class and never for a write, a `secretPath` route or a URL `scrubSecrets` would change (`retryHref`). The request id, one muted selectable line, is on a 5xx only. No path, no time.
- **The dock decides by session, once** (#662, `dock.tsx`): `Dock` renders nothing without `ctx.user`, so no signed-out page (sign in, register, the invite landing, the signed-out 404) carries five tabs that each bounce to the login page, and `:root:has(.dock)` drops the rail padding with it. Those pages show `BrandMark` (`brand-mark.tsx`: the app icon and name) above their content; their h1 is the page's own title (`LOGIN_TITLE`), the og title stays meta only (`AccountShell`'s `preview`). The sign-in forms' frame is `SignedOutFrame` (a `card`) around a `bare` `Fieldset`, not the fieldset's own border: a browser draws a legend straddling that border. The signed-in forms keep the bordered `Fieldset`. `SubmitButton` is `btn-primary` (`variant="error"` the only other).
- **View tabs and sheet closes have one writer each** (2026-10-18): a bar of link tabs is `LinkTabs` (`link-tabs.tsx`: a `<nav aria-label>` of links with `aria-current="page"`, never `role=tab`; one underline look and no class from a caller; the inactive colour is main.css's muted rule, #726; a `false` entry is a tab left out), a dialog's tap-outside close `SheetBackdrop` (`parts.tsx`, labelled Close on a sheet that shows, Cancel on one that asks a choice). `@link-tabs` and `@sheet-backdrop` (guards.conf) fail a hand-written copy.
- **Confirmations are the app's own sheet, never the browser's** (#686): `<ConfirmSheet>` (`confirm-sheet.tsx`, in every body) is driven by `public/js/confirm-sheet.js`, which answers `htmx:confirm` (`hx-confirm`) and the `submit` of a `PostForm` with `confirm` (`data-confirm`; a native post, held and resent with `requestSubmit` once confirmed), and exports `confirmAction()` (the outbox's sign-out discard imports it as `confirm-sheet`, an `--external` of `generate:outbox`). The destructive button is `btn-error` and wears the trigger's own words; Cancel is `autofocus`, Escape and the backdrop cancel, focus returns to the trigger. The module loads before `submit-once.js`, which skips a prevented submit. `@native-confirm` (guards.conf) refuses `confirm(` in `src/web`, `public/js` and `views/assets`; `pageErrors` (`test/support`) fails any scenario that sees a native dialog. **Reversible removals do not ask**: they act and `announce(reply, message, undo)` (`StatusUndo`: label, action, fields) puts an Undo button in the toast (`toast.js` posts the fields through `htmx.ajax`, 10 s); a calendar entry is the one so far (Calendar's Remove).

## Gotchas

- **htmx overwrites `history.state` with `{htmx: true}`** whenever it snapshots a page (before every boosted navigation, on every history restore), so anything kept on an entry must be written back after it: `back.js` keeps the trail on screen in a variable and rewrites it on `htmx:beforeHistoryUpdate` (the entry left), `htmx:pushedIntoHistory`/`replacedInHistory` (the new one) and `htmx:historyRestore`; a `popstate` listener reads `event.state` before htmx's handler clobbers it (htmx sets `window.onpopstate` at DOMContentLoaded, after the module registered). The arrow's click listener is on the document in the capture phase, ahead of htmx's listener on the boosted link.
- **The dock stacks above every page, and its active tab comes from one route table.** `.dock` and `.app-bar` are z-index 900 in `main.css` (daisyUI gives the dock 1, so the outfit card's `relative z-10` calendar button drew over it); page content stays at z-20 or below, the toasts at 999 above both. The bar and the dock never overlap, so they need no order between them. A bar fixed above the dock uses `bottom-dock` (`--spacing-dock` in `main.css`: the dock's height with the safe area); one whose height varies is `sticky bottom-dock` in the page's flow instead, so it never covers the page's end (Styling's, #664). A tab is lit on every page of its section: `sectionOf` (`src/web/layout/sections.ts`) maps the request path, whole segments (`/capsules/*` and `/laundry` are Wardrobe, `/wardrobe-share` is nothing). A new top-level route shows no tab until it is added there. `DOCK_TABS` is which sections the dock shows, in order; `SECTION_HOME` (the tabs' links) minus Today is the service worker's stale-while-revalidate tab roots (`OPENS_STALE`, `page-cache.ts`): a new tab root must render byte for byte the same while nothing changes, or be left out of it on purpose like Today. `test/dock.spec.ts (retired 2026-10-13)` probes the dock at 390 px on each page with floating controls.
- **A page's `<main>` needs `w-full` beside `max-w-* mx-auto`.** The body is `flex flex-col`, and a flex item with auto margins does not stretch: it sizes to its content, capped by the max width. A horizontal scroll strip (its max-content is every card) then widened the garment page to 512 px on a 390 px phone (#18b); short content made pages narrower than the screen. Every such `<main>` carries `w-full` since; the gallery's `sm:mx-auto` only applies past the phone.
- **At `lg` (1024 px) the dock is a left rail, from the same markup.** `main.css` restyles `.dock` (a 5rem column, the active mark on its left edge, links unchanged, so keyboard order is the dock's and `aria-current` marks the tab). Never a second markup or a width-dependent render: the server cannot know the viewport and the tab roots are cached byte for byte. Where the page has a dock, `:root:has(.dock)` sets `--rail-width` (5rem; 0 otherwise) and `--spacing-dock` to 0: the body pads left by the rail, `.app-bar` and the pull-to-refresh bar start at it, and anything fixed to the screen's left uses `left-rail` (the bars above the dock, the connectivity banner; the toast host centres on the content column). A new fixed bar uses `left-rail` and `bottom-dock`, never `left-0`. Dialogs (z 999) cover the rail; the rail is z 900 like the app bar.
- **Page widths come from `PageMain`, never a `max-w-*` on a page's `<main>`** (`page-main.tsx`; tokens `--container-page-narrow` 40rem, `--container-page-wide` 80rem in `main.css`). `narrow` is the default (forms, reading, single-column lists); `wide` is for galleries; `agenda` is `narrow` below lg and `wide` from it, for a list that becomes a grid on a desktop (the Calendar week, #467). Only the page-level wrapper chooses; dialogs, cards and controls keep their own `max-w-*`. Chosen so far: Calendar week `agenda`; the garment page and the garment form `narrow`; Today, Outfits (the saved grid is `OUTFIT_GRID`), Ideas, Styling, the Calendar month, Insights, the Wardrobe (`#wardrobe-main`), the plan page and the plans list `wide`; the plan change page and item form `narrow`. What sits outside `<main>` but must line up (the Wardrobe's tab strip) goes in `PageColumn` of the same width. Column steps are named once in `columns.ts` (`GALLERY_GRID`, `CARD_COLUMNS`, `OUTFIT_GRID`, `PAIR_GRID`), never copied `grid-cols-*`. The `FormPage` frames are `narrow` (the capsule, trip and "Bought it" forms widened from 32 to 40rem with it). Not yet converted (still `max-w-lg`/`sm:max-w-lg`, which works inside the rail): the other `<main>`s, the rest of Plans' pages, the trip and capsule pages, auth, sizes.
- **Fixed chrome never covers focus, and gives way to large text** (#730, `main.css`): `html` carries `scroll-padding-top` (the app bar, `--app-bar-h`, in rem so it and the two-line title clamp grow with the text, #1028) and, where a dock exists, `scroll-padding-bottom` (`--spacing-dock`, zero at the rail, plus 3.5rem when a `fixed bottom-dock` action bar is on the page, #1026: a new fixed bar taller than py-3 around a btn-sm updates that rule); below lg the dock is a size container and its labels become screen-reader-only under 22rem of container width, which in rem shrinks as the root font grows (a media query would not see a 200% root). The bar's h1 clamps to two lines instead of truncating; auth frames are `w-full max-w-xs`, never `w-xs`. `public/js/menu-escape.js` closes an open `.app-bar details` on Escape (a `<details>` has no key of its own). `test/trips.spec.ts` and `test/auth.spec.ts` assert each at 390 px.
- **A collage inside a link that already names the outfit is `decorative`** (#730: its photos `alt=""`; the calendar's rows and month cells, the week plan's and the garment page's thumbs). The Wardrobe's tab row is a `LinkTabs` `<nav aria-label>`, a landmark.
