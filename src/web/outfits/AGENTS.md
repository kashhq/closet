`src/web/outfits/` (ported to plain Fastify and Drizzle 2026-09-26). Outfits are private: every query is scoped to the signed-in owner, shares never reach them, `?ownerId=` is ignored, and another user's outfit id is a 404. **Every surface's read of one outfit and change to one is `outfit-actions.ts`'s** (#188d: `requireOutfitPage`, `requireOutfit` (get_outfit), `editOutfit` (the outfit form, Styling's Save in place, the API's PATCH, whose absent `slots` keep the rows: `OutfitEdit`), `removeOutfit` (a proposal not loved: 409), `reactToProposal`, suggest_outfit's `proposeOutfitTo`; `outfitNotFound` for Styling's `?outfit=` read), each `(db, actor, id, input)` over the owner-scoped queries with `actor.userId` and throwing `NotFound('outfit')` or a 409 with a `logDetail`, so routes and tools parse, call and render; a VIEW or MANAGE grantee is a stranger here (`outfit-actions.spec.ts`).

- **Composition is `outfit_slot`** and nothing else: `(outfit_id, position)` primary key, `category` text, `garment_id` nullable (`ON DELETE SET NULL`: deleting a garment empties its slot). Styling saves them top to toe (the rows' order); the outfit form old cached pages post keeps the order posted, and an empty slot is a row kept without a garment. A slot only ever names a garment of the outfit's owner: owned (closet or archived), or on the wishlist while nothing holds the outfit (see Incomplete outfits). Archived garments stay in outfits.
- **Composing is Styling** (#42, see Styling). The builder's pages are redirects: `GET /outfits/new` to `/styling` (carrying `?for=`, a pre-#13 `?scheduleDate=` as all day, `?capsule=`, `?returnTo=`) and `GET /outfits/:id/edit` to `/styling?outfit=ID` (its `returnTo=/calendar&returnToWeek=W` as `returnTo=/calendar?week=W`), so cached links and the manifest's old shortcut still land.
- **Origin and the one list rule** (#659, `src/wardrobe/outfit-origin.ts`, decisions 2026-11-13): `outfit.origin` is `person`, `agent`, `idea` (picked for a day, a trip or an entry: Wear this, a day's pick, Change) or `planner`; every `createOutfit`/`pickIdea` caller says it (the gallery's `pickOrigin`). A reuse only raises it (`adoptOutfit`), an edit makes it `person`. **Saved, the plan page and a trip's add page list only through `savedOutfitsSql(owner, list)`**, whose `keptOutfit()` (`person`, `agent`) is the one predicate; the rest are "Worn and planned" (`?show=one-offs`, `outfit-list.ts`, tabs in `outfit-list-tabs.tsx`, lenient; `GET /api/v1/outfits?show=`). Never a per-surface filter. A calendar row draws a one-off's collage and occasion, no name. `week-plan.spec.ts` (the one-offs) holds the three surfaces.
- **Muse's outfits** (#335, `proposals.ts`, `reactions.ts`, `muse-cards.tsx`): ordinary outfits the agent proposed, with the owner's reaction; which list each is on, the one writer, what may be held and the tab's cards: `muse-outfits.md`.
- **Incomplete outfits** (#335, the Muse epic; the rule and its two halves: `references.ts`): **an outfit may hold a garment not owned yet only while nothing holds it** (`outfitIsHeld`: no calendar entry, no trip), so a planned, packed or worn outfit is always complete. A slot write takes wishlist items offered to style with only into an unheld outfit (`slotMayNameSql`; into a held one it names them `GONE_WISHLIST_HELD`). Every write that makes an outfit held writes only where `outfitIsComplete` and reads `piecesToBuySql` in the same statement, throwing `OutfitIncomplete` (409, "Buy Wool blazer first: an outfit with pieces not bought yet can’t be planned or packed.") when it wrote nothing for that reason: the calendar's `upsertEntry`, `planToWear`, `setEntryOutfit`, and `addTripOutfit` (its check after the outfit's lock: Trips). Wearing needs an entry; Ideas, Today and the week planner pick closet garments only, and the generator's memory (`savedSlotsSql`) leaves incomplete outfits out (its duplicate rule compares drawn roles, so one whose piece to buy is an accessory would hide a closet idea). Derived on every read, never stored: Bought it completes an outfit, a dismissed pick keeps its slot. The views judge the garments they read (`OutfitGarment.status`, `piecesToBuy`; `to-buy.tsx`): the collage badges a piece To buy, the Saved grid's line says "1 piece to buy", the pickers (`SavedOutfitButton`, the Saved tab's `?for=day:`) disable it with "Buy 1 piece first", and its page shows the pieces above Edit in Styling (opened with Include picks on, `picks=1`), with no Plan. Styling's rows read the same rule (`rowGarmentsSql`), so a piece to buy stays as its row; `insertSlots` names its refusals itself (`slotRefusals`). MCP `list_outfits`/`get_outfit` answer `toBuy`. `incomplete-outfits.spec.ts` holds a row per surface.

## Gotchas

- **Never delete an outfit with a plain `DELETE`.** `outfit_calendar.outfit_id` and `garment_wear.outfit_calendar_id` both cascade, so it erases every wear of the days the outfit was worn. Go through `deleteOutfit`, which detaches them first (`detachOutfitWears`, one statement; Wears and washes). It runs under the owner lock, and the trips read and the delete are one statement (the read sees the `trip_outfit` rows the cascade then removes); a prune follows only when it was on a trip (#164). Deleting one calendar entry does cascade its wears, deliberately; account deletion takes everything. A new path that deletes outfits or calendar entries in bulk must choose between the two on purpose; `wears.spec.ts` covers both.
- **"Delete the outfit once nothing holds it" asks one predicate, `outfitIsHeld`** (`src/web/outfits/references.ts`: a calendar entry on any day, worn or not, and a trip's `trip_outfit`), like `photoIsReferenced` for photos. `deleteOutfit` cascades every holder, so a holder missing from the list is a row silently deleted with the outfit: until #69's review, `removeUnheldOutfits` (Undo, the daily re-plan's swap, Change) asked only `outfit_calendar` and took planner-made outfits off the trips that held them, packing lists included. A new table with an `outfit_id` joins the predicate in the same change, unless its link is `ON DELETE SET NULL` and losing it loses nothing (`plan_look.outfit_id`, #292, was one until `drizzle/0045_drop-plans.sql` dropped the plans, #337). `removeUnheldOutfits` locks the outfits (`FOR UPDATE`) before asking, so a holder being inserted meanwhile either committed first and is seen, or waits and fails on the deleted outfit's foreign key; never a silent cascade. `replace-entry.spec.ts` and `week-plan.spec.ts` hold a planner-made outfit on a trip through each of the three paths.
- **`createOutfit` is the one writer of new outfits, one outfit per garment set, always in `ownerTransaction`** (#219): every pick goes through `pickIdea` (`pick.ts`), and the form, MCP and the seed through `createOutfit`.
- **A save never drops a garment** (#219): one it cannot hold refuses the whole save, nothing written, the answer naming it (`OutfitGarmentsGone`): a 409 when every gone garment is the owner's, a 404 when any is not (indistinguishable, counted, never named).
- **Every list of outfits is read a page at a time through one reader** (#660, `outfitPageSql`, `OUTFIT_PAGE_SIZE`): newest first, keyset on the id, one row more to say there is a next.
- **Moved rules** (detail in `reference.md`): an outfit with no garments is never "the same", so `POST /outfits` refuses one. `slotMayNameSql` is the one rule of what an outfit may name, judged by `insertSlots` itself (locked `FOR SHARE`), never a read before. `readDayChoice` is the one judgement of a day for the plan page and the Saved tab. A feature never spells `?for=` itself or switches on its kind for a label. A collage's pieces shrink in width, never overflow; a long row scrolls inside its strip, never a first-N slice.
