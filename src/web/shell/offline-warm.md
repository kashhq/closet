# Warming the wardrobe for offline reading (#286)

Linked from `AGENTS.md` in this directory (PWA and the service worker). The design and its numbers: `docs/plans/2026-09-28-caching-and-offline.md`, sections 2 and 5. Read this before touching `WARM_PAGES`, `GET /offline/warm` or what the worker keeps for offline reading.

**What.** The installed app keeps the signed-in account's own wardrobe readable offline: next week (`/calendar?week=<next Sunday>`), every closet garment's page, every outfit's page, the Muse inbox (#333: `/wardrobe/wishlist`, which is no tab root, its open and chosen needs' decision screens, and each still-wanted wishlist garment's page and thumb, within `WARM_WISHLIST_CAP`, 60, added to the page and image caps; a set-aside one is only kept), the closet grid's later pages (`/wardrobe/tiles?before=`, as htmx requests, so under their `|hx` key) and every thumb those pages show. **Never the four tab roots** (`TAB_ROOTS`; owner decision on #287), so not this week's `/calendar` either: they open stale-while-revalidate, so a warmed copy, stored or refreshed behind the user's back, would open stale after their own edits (Back after a save served the warm's `/wardrobe`, `test/back-navigation.spec.ts (retired 2026-10-13)`). They are cached when opened, as before the warm; offline, one never opened is the offline page. The server leaves them out of the list and `parseWarmList` drops one if listed. Not warmed either (owner decisions on #178): cutouts (offline, a cutout never viewed is drawn from its thumb: `cutoutStandIn` answers a failed `/file/nobg/<name>?…` with the cached `/file/thumb/<name>?…`, the same query), selfies, Today, shared wardrobes. Also, since #657, every page that renders a queueable control (`isQueueablePage` in `src/offline-writes.ts`, read by `showsQueuedWrite` too): `/laundry` and the trips ahead or on (the last day not before today, nearest first, `WARM_TRIP_CAP`), listed right after next week so a tick or a wash queues offline without a prior visit. Writes stay online only, except the outbox's.

**The list.** `GET /offline/warm` (`routes.tsx`, `warm-list.ts`): session-gated, `no-store`, one statement (`selectScalars`: the closet and wishlist garments, the newest `WARM_OUTFIT_CAP` kept outfits through the Saved tab's paged `savedOutfitsSql` and the one-offs this week's and next week's entries hold (`heldOneOffsSql`), each with its garments: those one-offs are warmed first, #659; every other outfit only by id, `ownOutfitIdsSql`, for `keep`, #660). Always the session's own wardrobe, never `?ownerId=`, so warming never widens what a grantee's device holds. Caps: 300 garments, 80 outfits, newest first (`WARM_GARMENT_CAP`, `WARM_OUTFIT_CAP`, in `offline-warm.ts`), and at most `WARM_IMAGE_CAP` thumbs (the garments' first); the list names at most `WARM_PAGE_CAP` pages and fragments (next week, the capped garments and outfits, a grid page per `GRID_PAGE_SIZE` of them), which the integration spec checks at the caps. It answers `{ pages, fragments, images, keep }` (`WarmList`, `offline-warm.ts`, which the worker bundles; `parseWarmList` checks it at the worker's boundary) and says whose list it is in `X-Page-Account`. `keep` lists the garment, outfit and trip pages that still exist but are not warmed (wishlist items, pieces past the caps, trips past `WARM_TRIP_CAP` or ended): their visited copies stay. Only grid pages whose every tile is a warmed garment are listed (`gridPageCursors`), so offline the scroll stops at the last whole page rather than show tiles past the cap. The grid pages are built by `tilesUrl` (`wardrobe-page.tsx`), the sentinel's own builder, so the cached copy is the one the offline scroll asks for (`offline-warm.spec.ts` in `test/integration/` compares them).

**The run** (`runWarm`, `src-sw.ts`):

1. `pwa.js` posts `WARM_PAGES` on a signed-in page after `load`, on `controllerchange` (the app's first load has no controller until the new worker claims it), and again when the app comes back visible or online: never hidden, offline (the heartbeat's state) or with `saveData`. Asking is cheap: the worker decides.
2. The `warmed-at` record (`page-cache.invalid`, in `pages-v2`) gates it to once a day. A drop takes it with the cache, so the first page after a sign-in warms the new account, and one older than `activated-at` is due at once: a new worker's first page warms what the list has and the cache lacks, after the activation refresh has fetched again every copy an older build stored (`updates.md`; a refresh that stopped holds the warm back too).
3. The list is fetched (`X-Closet-Warm: 1`) and the session caches are claimed for its account (`claimPageCache`): another account's are dropped first. The worker's fetch is no navigation (`Sec-Fetch-Mode: cors`), so signed out the gate answers 401, never the login page; a 401 or `X-Session-Ended` (a revoked cookie, cleared by that very answer) is the session's end: `endFetchedSession` drops the session caches and the push subscription, but only while the generation it was asked in lasts.
4. Cached `/wardrobe/:id` and `/outfits/:id` pages and their `|hx` fragments (the key read back through `pageUrlOfCacheKey`; `isWarmedDetailPage`: no query, so never a shared wardrobe's) on neither `pages` nor `keep` are deleted: deleted or archived since. Under the ownership lock, and only while the run's generation lasts.
5. Due: a page without a copy, or one older than 24 h, another account's, or stored before this worker activated; a thumb not cached. Pages and thumbs alternate, two at a time, each marked `X-Closet-Warm: 1` (the server's request log says `(warm)`; the request histogram leaves it out, `src/metrics/AGENTS.md`), through the caches' own strategies: `pageWarmer` (`PageRefresh`: the network's copy or a failure, never NetworkFirst's cached fallback) over `pagePlugins`, and `images` (CacheFirst, `imageStore`). So every plugin applies: the 200 filter, `renderedAccount`, the owner claim, the stamp, and the generation check. **Each job carries the run's generation, not the one current when it starts** (`runOf`, read by `generationFor` in both plugins' `handlerWillStart`): the loop awaits between its generation check and a job's start, and a drop falling there would otherwise stamp the job with the next session's generation and store the previous account's page or thumb in it.
6. It stops at a drop (`the session changed`), at an answer that ends the session (`the session ended`, as in 3), at the first fetch that fails (`unreachable`: the next page resumes, skipping what is fresh), or once it has added 50 MB to the origin's `navigator.storage.estimate()` usage at its start (`storage budget reached`, read every 10 fetches, logged as a warning; unchecked on iOS 16, where the caps bound it). Photos visited before never stop a warm. `warmed-at` is written, under the lock and only in the run's generation, only when it ran to the end with every answer a 200 or a 404 (deleted since, `refused`; a page job's 404 also deletes that page's cached copies, `deleteCachedPages`, as step 4 does): a budget stop is not a completed run, so the next page warms on with a budget of its own, and any other error (a 500, a 503: `failed`) leaves it unrecorded too, logged as `[sw] warm incomplete, N failed: …`, so the next page fetches what failed.
7. One line: `[sw] warmed 31 pages, 12 images in 8.2 s (78 fresh, 2 removed, 0 refused, 0 failed, added 4.0 MB, usage 6.1 MB of 12 GB)`, or `[sw] warm stopped, <why>: warmed …`. A WARM_PAGES while a run is under way runs once more after it (the next account's first page after a drop). So does a run whose list a drop overtook (`fetchWarmList`: the claim finds the generation moved, e.g. the first signed-in page claiming the cache from the sign-in page's empty owner while the list was in flight): it sets the same `warmAgain`, since no WARM_PAGES may follow to ask again (#812; before, the device stayed unwarmed until its next load or visibility event). `trips.spec.ts` constructs it: `holdWarmList` wraps the worker's `fetch`, `dropSessionCaches` moves the generation, `warmOnce` is the run's only WARM_PAGES.

**Caps and storage.** `pages-v2` holds `WARM_PAGE_CAP` + 100 visited pages (488), `images-v2` `WARM_IMAGE_CAP` + 200 visited cutouts and photos (600), with no age limit (photo URLs are immutable, and an age limit only forced a refetch, a statement each): a warm at its caps never evicts what the user opened. **The offline page is never expired**: it is written by `offlinePage`, the page rules without the `ExpirationPlugin` (which only expires what it saw stored), so the oldest entry, stored at install, is never the fallback. The demo persona's warmed origin measured 4.4 MB in Chromium (2026-10-02; budget 10 MB). `pwa.js` calls `navigator.storage.persist()` once per launch in a standalone signed-in app and logs the answer: WebKit grants it to a Home Screen app by heuristics, which keeps the warmed caches out of eviction. In a Safari tab ITP's 7-day rule can still empty them: offline reading is dependable in the installed app only.

**Invalidation.** Online, every tap is NetworkFirst and replaces the copy, so a warm never makes a page shown online older. A copy read offline is at most about a day old and says so (freshness). A write made online leaves that garment's warmed full page stale until the next visit or warm.

**HTML caching.** `send()` (`src/web/render.ts`) gives every HTML answer `Cache-Control: private, no-cache` unless its route set one (the token page's `no-store`): no shared cache may store an account's page and the browser revalidates its copy (no-cache keeps the back-forward cache). `/file/**` and static answers are unchanged (`test/integration/delivery.spec.ts`).

**Specs.** `test/integration/offline-warm.spec.ts` (the list, no tab root in it, the grantee, the caps, the `(warm)` log); `test/offline-warm.spec.ts (retired 2026-10-13)` (the demo wardrobe offline after one warm, every page with its age and photos, no tab root warmed, the grid scrolled once `/wardrobe` was opened, a tab root never opened the offline page, under 10 MB; a second open warms nothing, a day later only the stale copy, a deleted garment's page removed; a new worker warms again within the day; a 503 leaves the run unrecorded); `test/stale-pages.spec.ts (retired 2026-10-13)`, "a warmed wardrobe and the session" (another account's sign-in empties both caches; a warm under way when the account changes stores nothing more). `stale-pages.spec.ts` also drops the session between a warm's fetches and during a thumb's (nothing of the account left in either cache) and revokes it elsewhere mid-warm (the 401 drops the caches). Specs whose caches must hold their own visits alone call `withoutWarming(context)` (`test/support/service-worker.ts`: the pages never post `WARM_PAGES`; a warmed-at record would not do, since every sign-in drops it).
