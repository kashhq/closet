# Wishlist

`src/wardrobe/status.ts`, `src/web/wardrobe/status.ts`, `src/web/wishlist/` (#18, slice 18a; plan section 11). A wishlist item is a garment with `status = 'wishlist'`: same table, form, photo, link import and sharing as any garment, and out of every closet read.

- **The status is a state machine.** `garment.status` (`wishlist`, `closet`, `archived`; a check constraint) replaced `archived boolean` in `drizzle/0014_garment_status.sql` (true to archived, false to closet; `garment-status-migration.spec.ts` (retired 2026-10-13)). Transitions are events, each with one edge: `buy` wishlist to closet, `archive` closet to archived, `restore` archived to closet (`garmentStatusTransition`, pure, `status.spec.ts` covers all nine pairs). Refused on purpose: wishlist to archived (dropping a wishlist item is a delete: it has no history to keep), anything to wishlist (wanting another is a new item: clone or "Find a replacement"), and self-moves. A garment is inserted with an entry status (`insertGarment`'s `EntryStatus`: closet or wishlist; a clone of a wishlist item lands on the requester's wishlist, anything else in their closet, `statusOfClone`) and changed afterwards **only by `setGarmentsStatus`** (`setGarmentStatus` for one; rows locked in id order, the machine asked for each, a purchase's day and price written with it, and its instant, `bought_at`, #337: the agent's feedback): archive (one, or select mode's, #677), restore, "Bought it" (`buyGarment`, which archives the replaced garment in the same transaction only when asked) and the seed.
- **`inCloset` is the one predicate of every closet read** (`src/web/wardrobe/status.ts`, with `onWishlist`, `ownedGarment` = closet or archived, and `inScope` for `GridFilters.scope`): the grid (`ownedGarment` with "Show archived"), its filter choices, the builder's cycles, capsules' counts, strips and pages, tagging, laundry and the wash counts, `availableGarment`/`isAvailable`, the MCP tools' closet reads, insights (#17). Writes that may only name owned garments filter by `ownedGarment`: capsule membership (`changeMembership`), `markWashed`, `setAway`; `setWoreToday` answers `'wishlist'`; the wear routes answer a wishlist item 409. Outfit slots follow their own rule, `slotMayNameSql` (Outfits, Incomplete outfits): an outfit nothing holds may hold a wishlist item offered to style with. The outfit generator (#9) reads `availableGarment`, which includes it; "Goes with my closet" (#18b) reads `closetGarmentsSql` (`inCloset`) and locks the wishlist item beside it (see below). `wishlist.spec.ts` proves an item is absent from each read.
- **Replacing**: `garment.replaces_garment_id` (nullable, `ON DELETE SET NULL`, not itself by a check), kept after "Bought it" as the record of what the purchase replaced. The same-owner rule is the writers': `replacementOf` (`src/web/wardrobe/queries.ts`) stores the id through a subquery in the insert or update itself, only when it names a garment of the same owner that is owned now or once, else null. It is read from the form only beside its marker `wishlist=1` (Gotchas). A garment marked `replace_soon` shows "Find a replacement" (owner and MANAGE) to `/wardrobe/new?to=wishlist&replaces=<id>`, prefilled from it (`destinationValues`: category, type, properties, brand, colours, size; not its name, notes, dates, price or care), and lists the wishlist items that would replace it.
- **Routes**: `GET /wardrobe/wishlist` (the Wardrobe's Wishlist tab in `wardrobe-header.tsx`, Muse's inbox since #333, its own routes in `inbox-routes.tsx`: see `suggestions.md`, The screens; under `/wardrobe`, so `sections.ts` already marks it), `GET|POST /wardrobe/:id/bought` (the day, today in `APP_TIMEZONE`, and the price paid, the listed one, prefilled; a checkbox to archive the replaced garment when it is still in the closet and the requester is its owner, unchecked; a native post re-rendered 400 on a bad date or price; 303 to the garment with `?bought=1`; an item no longer on the wishlist is a 409, its form a 302 to the garment). New items come from the garment form (`GET /wardrobe/new?to=wishlist`, posting `to=wishlist`; no care fields or acquired date on a wishlist form) from the link import, which carries `?to=wishlist&replaces=` from the link page to the form (`resolveDestination`, `src/web/wardrobe/destination.ts`; the share target stays the closet), and from a saved look's "Find this" (#22c, `addPieceToWishlist` in `src/web/inspiration/queries.ts`: `insertGarment` with the piece's kind and colours, the look in its notes, no product link).
- **Shared like the rest of the wardrobe** (owner decision on #18): a VIEW grantee reads the owner's wishlist (gifting; the owner reading a sibling's), a MANAGE grantee adds, edits and buys, only the owner archives what a purchase replaces (a grantee asking is a 403: `purchaseNeed` is ownership) and deletes an item (never a suggestion: 409, see `suggestions.md`). Matrix rows in `authorization-wardrobe.spec.ts`.
- **"Goes with my closet" (#18b; `src/wardrobe/goes-with.ts` pure, `goesWithCloset` in `src/web/gallery/ideas.ts`, `gallery/goes-with.tsx`)**: a wishlist item's page judges it against the closet, **the owner's alone** (it reads their closet and clashes, like ideas; a grantee who reads the item never gets it, and `goes_with_closet` refuses another's item: matrix rows in `authorization-wardrobe.spec.ts`, `mcp-seed.spec.ts`). The generator with the item `locked` (never in a pool, so ideas, Today and picks cannot draw it: `goesWithInputsSql` is the only read that hands one to the generator) over `closetGarmentsSql`: **the whole closet**, dirty and away included (a purchase is judged against what is owned, not today's laundry), **no weather** (worn across seasons; so no layer is drawn and each closet layer is checked on its own, locked with the item), **the item's own formality as the range** (the best few are those dressed at its level; the count still takes every outfit the hard rules allow), **no rotation** (idle days nulled: uniform draws). One search asks for a page as large as `MAX_DRAWS`, so it returns every distinct outfit it met: the count is exact below `OUTFIT_COUNT_CAP` (50) and "50+" past it; the best 3 as `OutfitCollage` cards (display only: no form, `pickIdea` takes closet garments; "Bought it" changes that); per role it is worn with, "k of n" and the best 3 partners (at its formality, then in the most outfits), layers always asked ("No layer goes with it"); **near-duplicates** (`nearDuplicates`: same category, same type, same colour set, not empty; brands ignored, where #20's "add a copy" asks it with `sameBrand`) warn "do I need this?", except the garment it replaces, shown like for like. Seeded by the item's id. The Muse inbox counts many items' outfits over one closet read (`unlocksOf`, `suggestions.md`). Theo's merino (replaces T21), padded jacket and Allbirds (twin of the Vejas) are the demo (`34-demo-goes-with`). ~15-35 ms over his 80 garments, so it renders inline; the page logs one debug line.
- **Muse's suggestions and option groups** (#333, epic #332; the design `docs/plans/2026-10-05-muse-suggestions.md`): a suggestion is a wishlist garment with provenance (`garment.suggested_at` and beside it), an option group one need holding its picks (`option_group`); deciding is feedback, so nothing is deleted, only dismissed. One writer each: `decide` (`decisions.ts`, every decision, through the pure `decideSuggestion`; a page's decision and the need page's read go through `suggestion-actions.ts`'s `makeDecision`/`requireNeed`, and Muse's writes through its `checkSuggestionRoom`, `markSuggestedPick`, `createNeed`, which authorize and refuse themselves, #188), `markSuggestion` (provenance, wishlist only) and `createOptionGroup` (a need, the agent's, #337); the agent's feedback is `feedback.ts`. Lists of things to buy read `wanted` (not set aside), closet reads `inCloset` as ever. The model, its migration from plans (`drizzle/0040`), and its specs: `suggestions.md` beside this file; read it before touching either table's columns.
- **"Bought it" is one owner transaction** (`buyWishlistItem(deps, actor, address, purchase)`, `purchase.ts`, which authorizes and refuses itself, #188): `buyGarment`, then `decide(bought)`, which settles a Muse pick's need and writes nothing for an own item. The owner lock comes first: `decide` takes it, and a pick takes it before its garments.
- **The wardrobe plans' addresses are retired** (#337 part B1, `inbox-routes.tsx`): `GET /wardrobe/plans`, `/wardrobe/plans/*` and `/wardrobe/shopping` answer 302 to the inbox, `GET /wardrobe/:id/plan-items` 302 to the garment (302, never 301: nothing caches them for good); an old form's post falls to a 4xx. `plans-removed.spec.ts`, which also sweeps every page for a plan link. `drizzle/0045_drop-plans.sql` dropped the plan tables (B2), their rows unmigrated (D10).
- **What the pages read, and why** (#167). The tab: one statement after the session (`readInbox`, `suggestions.md`). A wishlist item's page (#160, `src/web/wardrobe/garment-page.md`): the garment, then one statement for what it replaces, the brand note and "Goes with my closet"'s inputs (`goesWithInputsSql`: the item, the whole closet, the avoided pairs, as JSON); the closet and the item are read **without** the pool's last-worn subquery (`closetGarmentsSql`, typed `idleDays: null`): the search draws uniformly, so the per-garment `garment_wear` lookup fed nothing, and a grantee's Shuffle must not read the owner's wears anyway. The page's time is the search, not the database: about 5 ms counting outfits and 20 ms asking each of Theo's 11 layers on its own (`layerChecks`, a failing layer spends every draw). "Bought it"'s post reads nothing before `buyWishlistItem`, whose status change is the lookup (an unknown id is its `not-found`, the same 404), and the garment is read only to draw a refused form. The savepoint statements a purchase sends are `buyGarment`, `setGarmentStatus` and `decide` each opening their own transaction inside `buyWishlistItem`'s.
- **The API** (#194b; `api-routes.ts`, resources in `wishlist-resource.ts`, `src/web/api/AGENTS.md`): `GET /wishlist` is the inbox's sections as one resource, not paged (the needs sort by what every option unlocks: only the whole list knows); `GET /wishlist/needs/{id}` is C; `POST /wishlist/seen` is "New from Muse". Decisions are `makeDecision`'s: `POST /garments/{id}/decisions` (`event` `choose`, `dismiss` with a reason, `undo`), `POST /wishlist/needs/{id}/decisions` (`dismiss`, `undo`), `POST /garments/{id}/returns`; "Bought it" is `POST /garments/{id}/purchases` (`buyWishlistItem`; both fields explicit, null not recorded). A new item is `POST /garments` with `destination: wishlist`; "Bought a different one" is `POST /garments` with `boughtForNeedId` (`postedDestination`, the page's). `GET /garments/{id}/goes-with` (`goesWithCloset`) is the owner's: a share naming the owner is a 403 (coverage EXEMPT, a named test). Muse's own writes stay MCP's (`agentOnly`). `readInbox` reads the switcher only for a `viewerId` (the page's first page).

## Gotchas

- **A wishlist form's `replaces` has its own marker, `wishlist=1`.** Closet forms and forms cached before #18 post neither and must leave the stored replacement alone (the product, care and props markers' rule). The replacement is stored only through `replacementOf`, which is where the same-owner rule lives: a second writer of `replaces_garment_id` must go through it.
- **Logs** (#712, `docs/conventions.md#logging`): one line per write from its action, whichever door: `suggestion.decided` (`makeDecision`: `decision`, `garmentId`, `needId`, `dismissed`, `restored`), `suggestions.seen` (`markNeedsSeen`, only when something was new), `garment.bought` (`buyWishlistItem`: `archivedReplaced`, `needId`, `setAside`), and Muse's `need.created`, `garment.suggested`, `round.finished` (`suggestion-actions.ts`) and `outfit.proposed` (`outfit-actions.ts`).
