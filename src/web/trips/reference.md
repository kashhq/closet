# Trips: reference

Moved out of `AGENTS.md` (#723) so the auto-loaded doc keeps only invariants, gotchas and ownership. Not auto-loaded: read it before changing this area. Where it disagrees with the code or a spec, they win.

- **The API** (#194a; `api-routes.ts`, resources in `trip-resource.ts`, `src/web/api/AGENTS.md`): every page write has a twin through the same `trip-actions.ts` call (the destination and the forecast fragment's, `PUT /trips/{id}/destination` and `GET /trips/{id}/weather`, with WEATHER_ENABLED only, #195a; the place search is `GET /api/v1/weather/places`), each costing its page's statements (`api-statements.spec.ts`). The ticks are one row each, set-state (`PUT /trips/{id}/packing/{garmentId}`, `PATCH /trips/{id}/items/{itemId}`): `setPacked` answers `onList`, how many named ids the list holds, in its one statement, so a garment no longer on the list is a 404 the client drops; the page's queued checklist posts are unchanged.
- **Tables** (`drizzle/0024_trips.sql`): `trip` (owner, name, `destination` text, `latitude`/`longitude` rounded like the weather home and only with a destination (`trip_location_check`), `time_zone` the destination's IANA zone, only with them (`trip_time_zone_check`, 0057), `starts_on`/`ends_on` dates, at most `MAX_TRIP_DAYS` (60) by the form, notes); `trip_outfit` (trip, outfit, optional `day` and `occasion`, OCCASIONS by a check: the same outfit once a day, or once without a day, by two partial unique indexes, so adding again is `on conflict do nothing` and keeps the first occasion, the calendar's rule); `trip_item` (the extras: label once per trip in any case, `packed`); `trip_garment_packed` (trip, garment: a row means packed). All cascade with the trip; `trip_outfit` with its outfit, the mark with its garment.

- **One writer each** (`queries.ts`, every write under the owner's lock and then the trip's row lock, taken before any other, in one statement: `lockTrip`, or `lockedTripCte` for a write that is one statement, see Gotchas; the owner lock first is every synced write's, Calendar's Owner lock): `createTrip` (the bare insert the seed and fixtures write; the page and API create through `addTrip` (`trip-actions.ts`), over `createTripAt`, or with the API's `clientId` `createTripOnce` (a retry is 200 with the first trip, a deleted one's id a 410: `createOnce`, #717), which also locates a picked place and removes the planner's unworn auto entries on the trip's days from today on, `removeAutoEntries`, #684), `updateTrip` (new dates remove those entries too; a new destination name clears the location it no longer describes; outfits on days the new dates leave out move to "Any day", one row each, so `day` is always one of the trip's days), `setTripDestination` (the one writer of the place and its zone together: the page's post and the API's PUT, the zone from the search answer's hidden `timeZone`, and a create with a picked place, `createTripAt`, in the create's own transaction, #684; `updateTrip`'s rename clears all three; no MCP or sync writer; `trips.spec.ts`, `api-weather.spec.ts`), `deleteTrip`, `addTripOutfit` (a day outside the trip is refused, and an incomplete outfit, #335: `OutfitIncomplete`), `pickForTrip` (the gallery's pick and `plan_trip_outfit` with garments: `lockTrip` (the owner, then the trip), then `pickIdea`, whose owner lock is held already, then the trip outfit, one transaction), `removeTripOutfit`, `addTripItems`, `copyTripItems` (another trip's labels, unpacked, those already there skipped, so twice adds nothing), `setItemsPacked`, `removeTripItem`; `setPacked` (`packed.ts`: only garments on the list are marked). The form reads through `readTripForm` (`validation.ts`), which the seed posts through too.

- **The destination's forecast** (`forecast.ts`, `tripForecast`): the weather's own service and cache (`forecastFor`) at the trip's rounded location, its cache rows read in one statement with the person's offset and unit (`temperaturePrefsSql`: never their home or phone location, which a destination's forecast does not need) and handed to the service as the rows it knows, for its days the 16-day forecast reaches, each with what it asks of an all-day outfit (the person's offset; the rain gear applied is the water-resistant hat or cap **on the trip's packing list**, packed or not and whatever its state at home, `rainGearFor`'s `tripId`, which the trip's ideas pass too (`IdeasInput.tripId`, from `aimIdeas`), so forecast and ideas agree; a garment that left the closet (archived) is not gear: no such gear on the list and the day asks for water resistance, #759); later days say from when their forecast arrives and, meanwhile, show the destination's **typical weather** ("Typically 50–65 °F, 24% rain chance", under a note that it is the last 10 years' average and not a forecast; `data-typical-day`), from its climate normals (`normalsFor`, see Weather), each with what a typical day asks and its own Ideas link; nothing is fetched for a trip without a located destination or one that is over, and one that starts past the forecast fetches only the normals. A forecast Open-Meteo has not answered for the place marks the near days `unavailable` ("No forecast for the destination right now", `forecastUnavailable` in `get_trip`) and keeps the far days' typical weather (`forecast.spec.tsx`). Located through the weather's geocoding search on the trip page (`/trips/:id/places`, `WEATHER_SEARCH_LIMIT`, `secretPath`), set by `POST /trips/:id/destination` (`WEATHER_LOCATION_LIMIT`). **Weather-aware ideas**: the gallery with `for=trip:ID[:day]` passes the destination as `IdeasInput.place` (`ideasFor`), so a trip day's ideas match its weather (none, never home's, while the destination is not located); a day past the forecast is matched to the destination's typical day (`typicalDay`, the same `weatherNeeds` and `assessOutfit`), and the page says so ("Typically feels 52–65 °F (not a forecast)", `data-ideas-weather="typical"`, "Fits the typical weather"). Pages never render the forecast themselves: `GET /trips/:id/weather` is a fragment the page loads.
