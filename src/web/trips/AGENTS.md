# Trips

`src/wardrobe/packing.ts` (pure) and `src/web/trips/` (#10; plan section 4, the redesign's "Calendar › Trips"). A trip is **a standalone list of outfits** for some days away (owner decision: not a date range over the calendar) and the packing list derived from them. **The owner's own, like outfits**: no route takes `?ownerId=`, another user's trip, trip outfit or extra is a 404 with nothing written, shares never reach it (matrix rows in `authorization-trips.spec.ts`, a gallery pick for someone else's trip included).

- **What the home calendar knows of a trip is its days** (`away.ts`, #684): `awayTripsSql` (a scalar subquery folded into the statement a reader already sends: `weekContext`, Today's `readDay`, `planMyWeek`'s first read, the re-plan's second) and `splitAwayDays`. The week shows "Away: <destination, else name>" linking to the trip on each trip day and opens no slot there (`AwayLink`, `away-link.tsx`, also on Today); "Plan my week" skips those days (`away` in its answer, the banner's "Not planned while you are away"); the re-plan removes its own unworn entries there (Weekly auto-plan). A person's or an agent's entry on a trip day stays. Nothing else crosses: packed garments are not held back from home plans.

- **Who may reach a trip is `trip-actions.ts`'s** (#188e): every read of one trip and change to one, from the routes, the trips MCP tools, the gallery's pick and `aimIdeas`, takes `(db, actor, address, input)` over the owner-scoped queries with `actor.userId` and throws `NotFound('trip')` ("Trip not found"; a grantee of any permission is a stranger), `NotFound('outfit')`, the day's 400 naming the trip's dates (`NOT_A_TRIP_DAY`, the queries' `NotATripDay` carries them from the lock) or "Wore it"'s 409. Handlers and tools parse, call and render; `trip-actions.spec.ts` calls each as a stranger, a VIEW and a MANAGE grantee. Only the list and create, which refuse nothing, call the queries.

- **The API** (#194a; `api-routes.ts`, resources in `trip-resource.ts`, `src/web/api/AGENTS.md`): every page write has a twin through the same `trip-actions.ts` call (the destination and the forecast fragment's, `PUT /trips/{id}/destination` and `GET /trips/{id}/weather`, with WEATHER_ENABLED only, #195a; the place search is `GET /api/v1/weather/places`), each costing its page's statements (`api-statements.spec.ts`). The ticks are one row each, set-state (`PUT /trips/{id}/packing/{garmentId}`, `PATCH /trips/{id}/items/{itemId}`): `setPacked` answers `onList`, how many named ids the list holds, in its one statement, so a garment no longer on the list is a 404 the client drops; the page's queued checklist posts are unchanged.
- **Tables** (`drizzle/0024_trips.sql`): `trip` (owner, name, `destination` text, `latitude`/`longitude` rounded like the weather home and only with a destination (`trip_location_check`), `time_zone` the destination's IANA zone, only with them (`trip_time_zone_check`, 0057), `starts_on`/`ends_on` dates, at most `MAX_TRIP_DAYS` (60) by the form, notes); `trip_outfit` (trip, outfit, optional `day` and `occasion`, OCCASIONS by a check: the same outfit once a day, or once without a day, by two partial unique indexes, so adding again is `on conflict do nothing` and keeps the first occasion, the calendar's rule); `trip_item` (the extras: label once per trip in any case, `packed`); `trip_garment_packed` (trip, garment: a row means packed). All cascade with the trip; `trip_outfit` with its outfit, the mark with its garment.
- **The packing list is derived, never stored** (`packingList`, pure, `packing.spec.ts`; read by `tripModel`, `model.ts`, one statement): the distinct garments of the trip's outfits, grouped by role top to toe. **Copies needed**: a garment is worn once a day whatever the occasions (Day 2's daytime and dinner jeans are one wear), an outfit without a day is a wear of its own (`tripWears`); nothing is washed on the trip, so with a wash limit k the trip needs `ceil(wears / k)` copies, one for what is never washed (`copiesNeeded`); what can be packed is that up to the quantity owned. **Warnings**, only while the trip is ahead or on (a finished trip's list is a record): `too-few` (the trip needs more than owned), `wash` (up to departure: fewer clean copies now than to pack, from `availability.ts`'s rules and `wearsSinceWashSql`), `away`, `archived`.
- **Packed marks survive edits and leave with their garment.** Keyed by garment, so renaming the trip, adding an outfit or re-saving one that keeps the garment changes nothing. Every writer that can take a garment off a trip's list prunes the marks it orphans in its own transaction (`prunePacked`, `packed.ts`): `removeTripOutfit`, `updateOutfit` and `deleteOutfit` (`src/web/outfits/queries.ts`: an edit through `tripsOfOutfit`, a delete reading the trips in its own statement), and `setPacked` itself (in its one statement); a garment that comes back comes back unpacked. The reads join through the list, so a mark left by a race (an outfit edit beside a packing tap) never shows and goes at the trip's next write.
- **One writer each** (`queries.ts`, every write under the owner's lock and then the trip's row lock, taken before any other, in one statement: `lockTrip`, or `lockedTripCte` for a write that is one statement, see Gotchas; the owner lock first is every synced write's, Calendar's Owner lock): `createTrip` (the bare insert the seed and fixtures write; the page and API create through `createTripAt`; the API's create with a `clientId` through `createTripOnce` (a retry is 200 with the first trip, a deleted one's id a 410: `createOnce`, #717), which also locates a picked place and removes the planner's unworn auto entries on the trip's days from today on, `removeAutoEntries`, #684), `updateTrip` (new dates remove those entries too; a new destination name clears the location it no longer describes; outfits on days the new dates leave out move to "Any day", one row each, so `day` is always one of the trip's days), `setTripDestination` (the one writer of the place and its zone together: the page's post and the API's PUT, the zone from the search answer's hidden `timeZone`, and a create with a picked place, `createTripAt`, in the create's own transaction, #684; `updateTrip`'s rename clears all three; no MCP or sync writer; `trips.spec.ts`, `api-weather.spec.ts`), `deleteTrip`, `addTripOutfit` (a day outside the trip is refused, and an incomplete outfit, #335: `OutfitIncomplete`), `pickForTrip` (the gallery's pick and `plan_trip_outfit` with garments: `lockTrip` (the owner, then the trip), then `pickIdea`, whose owner lock is held already, then the trip outfit, one transaction), `removeTripOutfit`, `addTripItems`, `copyTripItems` (another trip's labels, unpacked, those already there skipped, so twice adds nothing), `setItemsPacked`, `removeTripItem`; `setPacked` (`packed.ts`: only garments on the list are marked). The form reads through `readTripForm` (`validation.ts`), which the seed posts through too.
- **"Wore it"** (`WornControl`'s create-and-wear: `POST /trips/:id/outfits/:tripOutfitId/wear`, else 409): only while the trip is on and only for an outfit of today or of no day (`wearableToday`, `packing.ts`, which the page's button reads too; a page left open past midnight must not mark yesterday's outfit worn today). Through the calendar's writers, `wearOutfitOn` (`src/web/calendar/queries.ts`: `planToWear` for today and the trip outfit's occasion, all day without one, which plans, takes over and locks the entry in one statement (an entry already there keeps its occasion), then `changeEntryWorn`, one owner transaction, idempotent), which Today's "Wear this" (`wearIdea`) uses too. The trip page shows "✓ Worn" and Undo for an outfit with a worn entry today (`outfitsWornOnSql` names the entry per outfit; `tripModel` keys it by the trip outfit and only for a copy wearable today, so the same outfit on another day is not marked; Undo is the calendar's `POST /calendar/:id/worn`, `worn=0`, back to the trip).
- **The destination's forecast** (`forecast.ts`, `tripForecast`): the weather's own service and cache (`forecastFor`) at the trip's rounded location, its cache rows read in one statement with the person's offset and unit (`temperaturePrefsSql`: never their home or phone location, which a destination's forecast does not need) and handed to the service as the rows it knows, for its days the 16-day forecast reaches, each with what it asks of an all-day outfit (the person's offset; the rain gear applied is the water-resistant hat or cap **on the trip's packing list**, packed or not and whatever its state at home, `rainGearFor`'s `tripId`, which the trip's ideas pass too (`IdeasInput.tripId`, from `aimIdeas`), so forecast and ideas agree; a garment that left the closet (archived) is not gear: no such gear on the list and the day asks for water resistance, #759); later days say from when their forecast arrives and, meanwhile, show the destination's **typical weather** ("Typically 50–65 °F, 24% rain chance", under a note that it is the last 10 years' average and not a forecast; `data-typical-day`), from its climate normals (`normalsFor`, see Weather), each with what a typical day asks and its own Ideas link; nothing is fetched for a trip without a located destination or one that is over, and one that starts past the forecast fetches only the normals. A forecast Open-Meteo has not answered for the place marks the near days `unavailable` ("No forecast for the destination right now", `forecastUnavailable` in `get_trip`) and keeps the far days' typical weather (`forecast.spec.tsx`). Located through the weather's geocoding search on the trip page (`/trips/:id/places`, `WEATHER_SEARCH_LIMIT`, `secretPath`), set by `POST /trips/:id/destination` (`WEATHER_LOCATION_LIMIT`). **Weather-aware ideas**: the gallery with `for=trip:ID[:day]` passes the destination as `IdeasInput.place` (`ideasFor`), so a trip day's ideas match its weather (none, never home's, while the destination is not located); a day past the forecast is matched to the destination's typical day (`typicalDay`, the same `weatherNeeds` and `assessOutfit`), and the page says so ("Typically feels 52–65 °F (not a forecast)", `data-ideas-weather="typical"`, "Fits the typical weather"). Pages never render the forecast themselves: `GET /trips/:id/weather` is a fragment the page loads.
- **Routes** (`routes.tsx`): `GET /trips` (the Calendar's Trips tab, `CalendarTabs`; `sections.ts` marks `/trips` as Calendar), `GET /trips/new`, `POST /trips` (303 `?created=1`; with WEATHER_ENABLED the form's destination is a picker, `DestinationPicker` in `form-page.tsx`: Find asks `GET /trips/new/places?destination=` (WEATHER_SEARCH_LIMIT) for the places, a pick swaps in `GET /trips/new/place` (the name read-only, the coordinates and zone hidden beside it) and Change `GET /trips/new/destination`, none writing; the post carries the pick (`NewTripBody`, all of it or none) and `createTripAt` stores it with the trip, so the page shows the forecast on first view; no pick, or a failed search, creates it unlocated and the page offers Find; the API's `place` likewise, #684), `GET /trips/:id` (days with their outfits in occasion order and "Any day", each with Ideas and "+ Add"; the packing list and the extras as `AutosaveForm`s posting the checked and `shown` ids, answering the status line and the summary out of band; notes), `GET /trips/:id/edit`, `POST /trips/:id`, `DELETE /trips/:id` (`navigateTo` the list; the outfits stay), `GET /trips/:id/outfits/new?day=&occasion=` (the calendar plan page's shape: day and occasion chips, Ideas, the saved outfits a page at a time as `SavedOutfitButton`s, the next from `/new/more?before=`, #660, Outfits), `POST /trips/:id/outfits`, `POST /trips/:id/outfits/:tripOutfitId/delete` and `/wear`, `POST /trips/:id/packed`, `POST /trips/:id/items`, `/items/packed` (these two ticks are queueable: a replay is a 204, see Wears' Queued writes), `/items/:itemId/delete`, `/items/copy`; with `WEATHER_ENABLED` only, `GET /trips/:id/weather`, `GET /trips/:id/places?q=` and `POST /trips/:id/destination`. Network first like every page but the tab roots; offline the worker's copy shows with its writes disabled (`data-needs-network`, a `data-offline-note`), except the packing and extras ticks, which queue per row (`offlineQueue`, `src/web/shell/outbox.md`), the new and edit form included (a post from a cached form would land on the offline page and lose what was typed).
- **Round trips** (#166; production pays ~114 ms a statement, #156; `trips.spec.ts`, "statements per request", holds them): the session, then one statement for each page (`GET /trips`, the trip page through `tripPageModel`, the edit form, the add page and its next page with its trip, a page of saved outfits and the trip's outfit days as `selectScalars` columns); the forecast fragment 3 (the trip, then the offset and cache rows). ## Gotchas

- **A writer that can take a garment off a trip's packing list prunes its packed marks** (`prunePacked`, `src/web/trips/packed.ts`, in the same transaction). The list is derived from the trip's outfits' slots, so today that is removing a trip outfit, editing an outfit's slots and deleting an outfit (its trips read before or in the delete: `trip_outfit` cascades). Styling's save (#42) edits through `updateOutfit` and creates through `pickIdea`, so it inherits it; a new path that changes slots or trip outfits (a bulk outfit delete) must call it too, or a garment that comes back to the list comes back already packed.
- **A trip write that is one statement reads the trip's rows as they were before it waited for the lock** (`lockedTripCte`, `packed.ts`: READ COMMITTED takes one snapshot per statement, and only the locked trip row is re-read). So a write joins that form only when its outcome does not hang on rows another trip writer adds under the lock meanwhile (the extras' unique labels are the index's; `addTripOutfit`'s dates are the locked row's; a mark `setPacked` races onto a garment that just left the list is one the reads never show). A write that judges the trip's outfits (`updateTrip`'s undating, `removeTripOutfit`'s prune) runs in a statement after the one that locks: one statement, an outfit added during the wait would keep a day outside the new dates. **A row read from outside the trip that a decision hangs on is locked in the statement**, so it is read again after the wait: `addTripOutfit`'s outfit (`owned`, `FOR KEY SHARE`; without it an outfit deleted during the wait passed the check and the insert died on the foreign key, a 500 instead of 'no-outfit', `trips.spec.ts` interleaves it). Every write of the statement reads `locked` (`exists (select 1 from locked)` is a one-time filter Postgres evaluates before the scan), so the trip lock still comes first. **What decides on the outfit's slots runs after the outfit's lock**: `addTripOutfit` locks the trip and the outfit in one statement and inserts in a second, where the outfit is complete (#335). One statement would judge the slots as they were before it waited for an edit's `FOR UPDATE`, and pack an outfit the edit just gave a piece to buy (`incomplete-outfits.spec.ts` interleaves both orders).
