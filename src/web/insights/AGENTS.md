How the closet is actually used, computed on every request from the wear log; nothing stored. **The owner's own, like wears**: `GET /wardrobe/insights[?unworn=30|90|180]` (in the Wardrobe's ⋯ menu; under `/wardrobe`, so the dock marks Wardrobe) ignores `?ownerId=`, and a share never reaches it (matrix row in `authorization-outfits.spec.ts`). `?unworn=` is navigation state: anything else is 90, never a 400 (no length limit either: one answered a long value 400 before the fallback, #123).

- **Two statements** (`queries.ts`, run together by `readInsightRows`; `readInsights`, which the page and `wardrobe_stats` share, reads them over `recentWindow(today)`): `insightGarments`, one row per garment in the window's scope (the closet, `inCloset`) with its wear days ever, in the window (the last year), the last day worn, its repairs' cost up to `to` (`repairCostSql`, a scalar subquery, #151), `acquired_on`, and days since it and since `acquired_on` (Postgres `date - date` against the window's last day, today, so the pure side does no date arithmetic); and `wornPairs`, the self-join of the window's distinct (garment, day) pairs, at most 5. `InsightsWindow` (`from`, `to`, `scope`) is what the recap changes: no wear after `to` is read (a join condition, so a past year stops at its December 31). `wardrobeInsights` computes everything else from those rows. The page is the owner row plus these two (`recordQueries` in `insights.spec.ts` and `insights-seed.spec.ts`): **a new figure is computed from the rows in `insights.ts`, not a new query**, unless it needs data the rows lack.
- **Definitions** (`insights.ts` states them; the specs pin each on a known closet): the closet is `inCloset` garments, each counted once whatever its quantity (worn when any copy was); wears are distinct days. _Worn in the last N days_ (30, 90, 365): a wear today or in the N - 1 days before. _Unworn in N days_ (30, 90, 180): never worn or last worn N days ago or more, among garments owned at least N days (no `acquired_on` counts as owned long enough), never worn first, then the longest since; each with "Style this" (`styleThisUrl`, `urls.ts`: Styling's `/styling?with=` (#42), as on the garment page). **Every garment list on the page is at most five rows** (#728, `RANKED_LIMIT`): the ranked ones are cut there by `insights.ts`, and the unworn list, whole, shows its first five and "See all" (`data-see-all`) to `GET /wardrobe/insights/unworn[?unworn=]` (`UnwornPage`, `unwornListUrl`: the same read, every garment of the list uncapped, its chips staying on it). The breakdowns (colours, categories, brands) are shares of the whole closet and stay whole. _Most worn_: by wear days ever, ties the most recent first. _Least worn_: among garments owned `LEAST_WORN_MIN_OWNED_DAYS` (90) or more, leaving out the most worn. _Cost per wear_: `price` is per piece and, after "Bought it", the price paid (it overwrites the listed one), so the cost (`totalCost`, every surface's one definition) is price × quantity plus the repairs' costs (#151; once, not per copy), divided by wear days, in cents rounded to the cent; no price is left out (counted), however much its repairs cost; "Your closet cost" sums the same costs and says "repairs included"; 0 wears is "not worn yet" and never divided; best is the cheaper half at most (up to 5), worst the dearer half, so no garment is in both. _Pairs_: two closet garments worn on the same day (whichever entries held them) on at least 2 days of the last year. _Colours_: each colour's share of the whole closet's garments and of all the last year's wear days, a garment in k colours counting 1/k to each and one without a colour to `uncoloured`, the strip's plain end (strips of `.ms-swatch--<colour>` segments grown by `flex-grow`; until #123 the shares were of the coloured garments only, so a half-untagged closet read as its colours at 100%). _Categories and brands_: garments, pieces (quantity), share of the closet and of the year's wear days; brands grouped regardless of case and spaces under the first-added garment's spelling, 8 named and the rest summed. _Condition_: needs_repair and replace_soon counts, linking to the grid's "Needs attention" filter (`?attention=true`).
