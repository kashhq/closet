- **Year in review** (#26, `docs/plans/2026-09-28-yearly-recap.md`): `GET /wardrobe/recap[?year=YYYY]` (`recap-page.tsx`, `recap-period.ts`, pure `src/wardrobe/recap.ts`), in the Wardrobe's ⋯ menu (on one's own wardrobe only) and linked from insights. The same two statements over a calendar year (`from` January 1, `to` December 31 or today, `scope: 'owned'`: a garment archived since was still that year's; a repair counts by its day like a wear: every one up to `to`, before the year too, as the price is the whole price, so a past year's cost per wear is what it was on its December 31), then `yearRecap` with insights' rules (`costFigures`' best, `colourShares`' worn shares, the pairs), no statement of its own (`recap.spec.ts` counts them). **Owner-only, like insights**: the wear log never reaches a grantee, so `?ownerId=` is ignored (the schema strips it) and a grantee passing the owner's id sees their own recap (`recap.spec.ts`; matrix row in `authorization-outfits.spec.ts`). "Save image" is therefore always one's own. A first version was share-aware; the coordinator reversed it on #26. The year boundary is `todayIn(APP_TIMEZONE)`'s (`today.spec.ts` has the New Year's Eve row); `?year=` is navigation state (not a four-digit year up to this one: this one); without it, a current year with fewer than `RECAP_MIN_WEARS` wears opens on the latest earlier year that has enough (`readDefaultRecap`, three more statements only then: the year lookup and the earlier recap's two, 6 in 4 round trips, and the chosen year's own `enough` is checked, `recap.spec.ts` holds the lookup's count to the recap's; none: this year's empty state; the API stays on its explicit year, #733). Fewer than `RECAP_MIN_WEARS` (10) wears is the empty state; a year's additions are `acquired_on` in it (undated garments are never additions). ‹ shows only when something was worn before the year (`earlier`: lifetime wears beyond the year's), › only for a past year. **"Save image" is drawn on the device** (`public/js/recap-export.js`, the importmap's `recap-export`): Canvas 2D from the button's `data-card` JSON (an attribute, since htmx drops a `<script>` island from a boosted page, #672; every string already translated; mounted by `page-mount.ts`), the page's fonts, the light theme's tokens from a hidden `[data-theme="closet-light"]` probe (the card is light in dark mode too), the strip's segments for the swatch colours and the same-origin thumbs; drawn as the page opens, the button enabled once the PNG exists, so the tap calls `navigator.share({ files })` inside its user activation (iOS), else downloads; a card that cannot be drawn (a font that will not load only falls back) says so in `#recap-export-note` (aria-live) and leaves the button enabled to draw again. `test/recap.spec.ts (retired 2026-10-13)` reads the PNG back (1080 × 1350, light) and forces the failure (`toBlob` answering null). Why not sharp on the server: the slim image has no fonts, and Pango ignores the woff2 we ship (the plan says more).
